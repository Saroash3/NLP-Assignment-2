# Dress Code

Zor Fitness Club's dress code keeps members safe around moving machines and weights, and keeps the club comfortable and respectful for everyone.

## Required everywhere on the gym floors
- **Athletic wear**: tracksuits, gym trousers, shorts to the knee, t-shirts, sports tops
- **Closed sports shoes** (trainers or cross-trainers). Shoes must be clean. Many members carry a separate pair so they don't bring in mud during the monsoon.
- **A personal towel.** It is **mandatory** on the gym floor, to wipe equipment and for hygiene. Forgot yours? Rent one for **PKR 300**. Elite members get 1 free towel per visit.

## Not allowed on the gym floors
- **Jeans** or trousers with rivets, which damage the upholstery
- **Chappals, sandals, khussa, slippers or formal shoes**, and bare feet (except on yoga or Pilates mats)
- **Shalwar kameez on the gym floor.** Loose fabric can get caught in treadmills, cable machines and bike wheels. **Shalwar kameez IS allowed** in the yoga and Pilates studio classes (Yoga Flow, Prenatal Yoga, Pilates).
- Belts, keys or jewellery with sharp edges that could scratch equipment

## Main floor: extra rules for men
- A **shirt or t-shirt must be worn at all times**. **No bare chest**, and **no cut-off "stringer" vests**. Sleeveless sports vests with a normal cut are fine.
- Shorts must reach at least mid-thigh
- A **modest dress code** applies on the main floor at F-7 and Bahria, which are used by men and by women on mixed plans. Please avoid see-through or very revealing clothing.

## Ladies Floor, Ladies Wing and G-11 ladies hours
- Any athletic wear is fine
- **Sports hijab, full-length leggings and long athletic tops are welcome**, and many of our members train this way
- **Abaya:** please remove it before using treadmills or cable machines. There are hooks and lockers in the changing rooms.
- The same shoe and towel rules apply

## Pool (F-7)
- A **swim cap is mandatory** for everyone
- Proper swimwear only. Burkinis and full-length swimsuits are welcome in the ladies session and family swim. No cotton clothing or shorts that aren't swimwear.
- Shower before entering the pool

## Steam and sauna
Swimwear or a towel wrap. **No gym shoes** inside.

## Classes
- **Zumba, HIIT, spin, boxing, circuit:** closed sports shoes
- **Yoga, Pilates:** barefoot on the mat is allowed, and shalwar kameez is fine
- **Boxing:** bring your own hand wraps

## Enforcement
Front-desk staff can refuse entry to the floor, or ask a member to change, if they are not dressed correctly. Repeated refusal to follow the dress code is treated as a conduct issue. Sports shoes and towels are sold or rented at every front desk.
