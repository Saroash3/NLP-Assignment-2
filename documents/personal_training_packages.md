# Personal Training (PT) Packages

One-to-one coaching with a Zor Fitness Club trainer, at any branch where that trainer works.

## Prices (same for every trainer, male or female)
| Package | Price | Per session |
|---|---|---|
| 8 sessions | PKR 18,000 | PKR 2,250 |
| 12 sessions | PKR 25,000 | about PKR 2,083 |
| 20 sessions | PKR 38,000 | PKR 1,900 |

- Each session is **60 minutes**
- Packages are **valid for 60 days** from the date of purchase. Unused sessions expire after 60 days.
- Packages are **non-refundable**
- Sessions can be **moved between Zor trainers**. For example, you can use 4 with Hamza Qureshi and 4 with Ali Raza. They **cannot be transferred to another member**.
- **Elite members** get **4 PT sessions per month included**. These don't roll over, and extra sessions can be bought as a package.

## Cancellation rules
- **Cancel at least 12 hours before** your session in the app or on WhatsApp. Otherwise the **session is forfeited** and counts as used.
- **If your trainer is more than 10 minutes late**, you get that session **free**. It is added back to your package.
- PT can't be booked 1:00pm–2:15pm on Fridays (Jumu'ah).

## Rules on outside trainers
**Outside and freelance trainers are not allowed** at any Zor branch. A member caught coaching another member for money has their membership **terminated without refund**. Friends training together and giving each other tips for free is fine.

## No home training
Zor does **not** offer home training. All PT sessions take place at our branches.

## Buying a package
Buy at the front desk or in the app. Pay by cash, card, bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa. Non-members can't buy PT. You need an active membership (any plan), because PT happens on the gym floor. Standard members train with a trainer at their home branch only.
