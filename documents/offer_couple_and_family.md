# Couple and Family Discounts

Training together is easier to stick with. Zor Fitness Club offers two discounts for households.

## Couple discount: 10% off each partner
- For **married couples** where **both** partners join the **Plus** or **Elite** plan
- The partners can be on different plans. For example, the husband on Elite and the wife on Plus both get 10% off.
- Show your **nikahnama** or a family registration certificate **once** at joining
- Prices per month: Plus **PKR 14,400** each, Elite **PKR 23,400** each
- Not available on Standard or Ladies Only
- If one partner doesn't renew, the other moves to the normal price at their next renewal

## Family discount: 15% off each member
- For **3 or more members of the same household**, such as parents with grown-up children, siblings, or in-laws living in the same house
- Every family member gets **15% off any plan**. Members can choose different plans and different home branches.
- Only **one PKR 10,000 admission fee** is charged for the whole family
- Monthly family prices: Standard at F-7 or Bahria **PKR 10,200**, Standard at G-11 **PKR 6,800**, Plus **PKR 13,600**, Elite **PKR 22,100**, Ladies Only **PKR 8,500**
- Proof of the same household: a family registration certificate or CNICs showing the same address
- If the household drops below **3 active members**, the family discount stops at each remaining member's next renewal

## Example family
A mother on Ladies Only at the F-7 Ladies Floor, her husband on Plus, and their 19-year-old son on Standard at G-11. They pay 15% off each (PKR 8,500 + PKR 13,600 + PKR 6,800 per month) and one admission fee.

## Teenagers in the family
- Aged **16–17**: can use the gym floor with a parent's signed consent
- Aged **13–15**: supervised group classes only, with signed consent
- The kids' play area on the F-7 Ladies Floor is free for children aged **3–8** of **any female member** while she trains there, for a maximum of 90 minutes per visit.

## Family swim
The F-7 pool has a **family swim on Sundays 10:00am–2:00pm**. Children under 12 must be in the water with a parent. The pool is included for Elite members, and Ladies Only members can use the family swim. Other members pay PKR 1,500 per swim.

## No stacking
A family member who is also a student chooses **either** the 25% student discount (with off-peak entry only) **or** the 15% family discount. They can't have both. The same applies to corporate, senior and couple discounts.
