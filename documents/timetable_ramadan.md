# Ramadan Class Timetable

During Ramadan the normal 17-class timetable is **paused**. Only **5 classes** run, all in the **night session after taraweeh**, when members have eaten and rehydrated.

## Ramadan night classes
| Class | Branch / area | Days | Time | Trainer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Zor Circuit | G-11 (men) | Mon, Wed, Fri | 10:00–10:45pm | Usman Tariq |
| HIIT Blast | Bahria main floor | Tue, Thu | 10:30–11:15pm | Bilal Khattak |
| Spin Night | Bahria spin studio | Mon, Wed, Fri | 11:00–11:45pm | Zeeshan Haider |
| Ladies Yoga Flow | F-7 Ladies Floor | Tue, Thu | 9:30–10:15pm | Mahnoor Rizvi |
| Ladies Zumba | Bahria Ladies Wing | Tue, Thu | 9:30–10:15pm | Farah Naz |

Notes:
- **Spin Night moves to Mon/Wed/Fri** in Ramadan. In normal months it runs Tue/Thu 7:30–8:15pm.
- **HIIT Blast runs only Tue/Thu** in Ramadan, with no Saturday class.
- **Ladies Zumba at Bahria stays on Farah Naz's normal Bahria days (Tue/Thu)**, just later at night.
- There are **no day-session classes** during Ramadan. The day sessions (men 10:00am–3:00pm, ladies 11:00am–3:00pm) are for open gym only.

## Classes that do NOT run in Ramadan
Strength Foundations, Powerlifting Club, Boxing Conditioning, Muscle Building Clinic, Ladies Strength & PCOS Program, Prenatal Yoga, Pilates, the F-7 Zumba, Ladies Zor Circuit, Ladies Stretch & Mobility, the morning Spin and Sunday Long Ride. They restart on the first working day after the Eid-ul-Fitr closure (Eid days 1–2).

## Ramadan night-session hours
- Men / main floors: **9:30pm–2:00am**
- Ladies Floor, Ladies Wing and G-11 ladies: **9:00pm–12:00am**
- G-11 and the Bahria Ladies Wing remain **closed on Sundays**

## Booking in Ramadan
- The same rules apply: book in the app or on WhatsApp up to 48 hours ahead (Elite 72 hours), and cancel at least **3 hours** before or it counts as a no-show.
- **3 no-shows in a calendar month** means a 7-day booking block. This still applies in Ramadan.
- Classes with **fewer than 3 bookings** 2 hours before the start may be cancelled. Night classes are more likely to be cancelled on the last 10 nights of Ramadan, when many members attend itikaf or late prayers, so check the app.
- Capacity is unchanged: spin 24, Zumba 25, others 15.

## Who can join?
- Plus, Elite and Ramadan Fit Pass members: all Ramadan classes
- Ladies Only members: Ladies Yoga Flow and Ladies Zumba
- Standard members: drop-in at PKR 1,000 per class

## Intensity advice
Even at night, keep intensity moderate in the first week of Ramadan while your body adjusts. Drink water between iftar and sehri, and tell the trainer if you feel dizzy. Bilal Khattak's HIIT Blast offers a lower-impact option in every round.
