# Kids' Play Area (F-7 Ladies Floor)

Finding time to exercise is hard with young children. The kids' play area lets mothers train while their children play safely nearby.

## Where
Inside the **F-7 Islamabad Ladies Floor** (2nd floor, College Road entrance). **It is the only kids' area at Zor Fitness Club.** There is **no kids' area at G-11 or Bahria**.

## Who can use it?
- It is **free for the children of ANY female member**, on **any plan**: **Standard, Plus, Elite or Ladies Only**
- **Condition:** the mother must be **training on the F-7 Ladies Floor** during that time
- Children aged **3 to 8 years**
- **Not available** to male members, guests, or day-pass holders
- A female Standard member can use it only if her home branch is F-7, because Standard gives access to one branch only

## Hours
- Supervised **10:00am–4:00pm**
- In **Ramadan**: day session only, **11:00am–3:00pm**
- Closed on Fridays **1:00pm–2:15pm** (Jumu'ah), with the rest of the club
- Open on Sundays when the Ladies Floor is open, 10:00am–4:00pm (the Ladies Floor itself closes at 6:00pm on Sunday)

## Rules
- **Maximum 90 minutes** per visit
- **The mother must stay in the building** for the whole visit. She can't leave for shopping at Jinnah Super Market while her child is in the play area.
- Sign in at the Ladies Floor desk with your child's name and age, and a phone number
- Children must be **toilet-trained**. Staff do not change nappies.
- Children who are unwell (fever, cough, stomach upset) can't attend
- Bring a water bottle and a small snack in a closed box, labelled with the child's name. No nuts, because of allergies.
- **Only the mother** (or a named female guardian registered at the desk) can collect the child

## What's inside
- Soft play mats and foam blocks
- Books, puzzles, colouring and building toys
- A small quiet corner for younger children
- A trained female supervisor at all times. The maximum is 8 children at once, first come first served.
- **No screens**: we keep it a TV-free, tablet-free zone

## Privacy
Like the rest of the Ladies Floor, **no photography or filming** is allowed in the kids' area.

## Tips for mothers
- Mid-morning (10:00am–12:00pm) is the quietest time
- The Ladies Strength & PCOS Program (Mon/Wed/Fri 11:00am–12:00pm) and Yoga Flow (Tue/Thu 10:00–11:00am) both fit inside the 90-minute limit, along with a short warm-up
- The ladies pool session (Mon–Sat 11:00am–3:00pm) is on the same schedule. Children in the play area can't go into the pool with you, but the family swim on Sunday is open to all ages.
