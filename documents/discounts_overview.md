# All Discounts and Offers at a Glance

Zor Fitness Club has several discounts. The most important rule: **only one discount can apply at a time. Discounts never stack.** If you qualify for more than one, pick the one that saves you the most.

## How "no stacking" works
- **Prepaid and personal discounts:** the student, couple, family, corporate and senior discounts are applied to the monthly price **instead of** the prepaid 10/15/25% discount. However, the admission fee waiver on 6 and 12-month terms still applies.
- The **referral reward** is not a price discount, so you can earn referral weeks while on any plan or discount.
- The **free trial** is available to everyone, whatever discount they later choose.

## Examples
- A family of 4 where the father's company is on the corporate scheme: the father can take the corporate 15% or 20% instead of the family 15%. The other 3 members still count as a family, since 3 or more are active.
- A senior aged 60+ who wants Plus: the senior discount only covers Standard, so Plus is at the normal price unless the family or corporate discount applies.
