# Cardio Classes: Spin, HIIT Blast and Boxing Conditioning

## Spin (morning)
- **When:** Monday, Wednesday, Friday, **6:30–7:15am**
- **Where:** Bahria Town Phase 7 spin studio (24 bikes)
- **Coach:** **Zeeshan Haider**, who has completed the Lahore Marathon 3 times
- **Level:** all levels. You control the resistance on your own bike.
- **Capacity:** 24, one person per bike
- A 45-minute ride with music, hill climbs and sprints. It is a good way to start the day before the office.

## Spin Night
- **When:** Tuesday and Thursday, **7:30–8:15pm** (in Ramadan it moves to Mon/Wed/Fri 11:00–11:45pm)
- **Where / coach:** Bahria spin studio, Zeeshan Haider
- **Level:** all levels. It is the most popular class at Bahria and often has a waitlist, so Elite members use their 72-hour booking window.

## Sunday Long Ride
- **When:** Sunday, **11:00am–12:00pm**. It is the only class on Sundays.
- **Where / coach:** Bahria spin studio, Zeeshan Haider
- **Level:** **intermediate.** It is a 60-minute endurance ride, so you should be comfortable with a 45-minute spin class first.

## HIIT Blast
- **When:** Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday, **6:00–6:45pm** (in Ramadan, Tue/Thu 10:30–11:15pm only)
- **Where:** Bahria main floor and boxing zone
- **Coach:** **Bilal Khattak**, a former amateur boxer from Peshawar
- **Level:** **intermediate.** It is 45 minutes of intervals: kettlebells, battle ropes, bodyweight moves and heavy-bag rounds. Every round has a lower-impact option.
- **Capacity:** 15

## Boxing Conditioning
- **When:** Tuesday and Thursday, **8:00–9:00pm**
- **Where:** **F-7 Islamabad main floor**. Bilal comes to F-7 for this class after HIIT Blast at Bahria.
- **Coach:** Bilal Khattak
- **Level:** all levels. It covers basic punches, footwork, pad work in pairs, and conditioning. There is no sparring.
- **Bring:** your own hand wraps. Gloves are provided, but many members buy their own for hygiene.

## Health requirements
Members with a **heart condition** must bring a **doctor's note** before joining HIIT Blast or any Spin class. Everyone should declare conditions on the PAR-Q form at joining. Staff are first-aid trained, and there is an AED at every branch.

## What to wear
Athletic wear and **closed sports shoes** are required. Spin bikes use normal trainers, and no cleats are needed. A **towel is mandatory**, and in spin you'll need it. Bring a water bottle, because there are no plastic cups at the water dispensers.

## Access
Plus and Elite: free. Standard and non-members: PKR 1,000 drop-in. All these classes are held on the main floors and are open to men and women on mixed plans. Ladies Only members can't join them, because they are not ladies-only classes. Student members can attend morning Spin (Mon/Wed/Fri 6:30am) only.
