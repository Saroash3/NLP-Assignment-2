# Ramadan Timings and Rules

During Ramadan, Zor Fitness Club switches to a **split schedule**, a day session and a night session, so members can train before iftar or after taraweeh.

## Ramadan opening hours (all branches, every day except Sunday rules below)
| Area | Day session | Night session |
|---|---|---|
| **Men / main floors** (F-7, Bahria, G-11 men) | **10:00am–3:00pm** | **9:30pm–2:00am** |
| **Ladies:** F-7 Ladies Floor, Bahria Ladies Wing, G-11 ladies | **11:00am–3:00pm** | **9:00pm–12:00am** |

- **G-11** stays **closed on Sundays**, and the **Bahria Ladies Wing** stays **closed on Sundays**, even in Ramadan.
- The **Friday prayer break (1:00pm–2:15pm)** still applies to the day session.
- **Last entry** is 45 minutes before each session closes.

## What changes in Ramadan
- **The pool is closed** for the whole month.
- **Steam and sauna open in the night session only.** Using them while fasting is not allowed, because of the dehydration risk.
- **Most classes stop.** Only the Ramadan night timetable runs:
  - Zor Circuit, G-11, Mon/Wed/Fri 10:00–10:45pm (Usman Tariq)
  - HIIT Blast, Bahria, Tue/Thu 10:30–11:15pm (Bilal Khattak)
  - Spin Night, Bahria, Mon/Wed/Fri 11:00–11:45pm (Zeeshan Haider)
  - Ladies Yoga Flow, F-7 Ladies Floor, Tue/Thu 9:30–10:15pm (Mahnoor Rizvi)
  - Ladies Zumba, Bahria Ladies Wing, Tue/Thu 9:30–10:15pm (Farah Naz)
- **PT sessions** can be booked in either session, depending on each trainer's Ramadan roster, which is shown in the app.
- The **kids' play area** at F-7 runs only in the day session, 11:00am–3:00pm, because it is inside the Ladies Floor.
- The **Zor Juice Bar and Café** open only in the night session, serving shakes, dates and light snacks after iftar.

## Ramadan Fit Pass
New members can join during Ramadan with **1 month of Plus for PKR 11,000** and **no admission fee**. It is one per CNIC and can't be frozen.

## Freezing in Ramadan
Ramadan is **not** an automatic freeze. Members who don't want to train can use their normal freeze allowance (3-month plan 7 days, 6-month 15 days, 12-month 30 days), requested 48 hours ahead. Monthly plans can't be frozen.

## Training advice while fasting
- **Before iftar (day session):** light cardio, walking, stretching and technique work. Keep sessions under 45 minutes.
- **After taraweeh (night session):** strength training and intense classes, once you have eaten and rehydrated.
- Kamran Baig offers **sehri and iftar meal plans** (PKR 4,000 per consultation, or included monthly on Elite).
- Stop immediately if you feel dizzy. Staff are first-aid trained, and each branch has an AED.
