# Standard Membership Plan

Standard is Zor Fitness Club's entry-level plan: gym floor access at one branch, without classes, pool or steam.

## Price
| Branch | 1 month | 3 months | 6 months | 12 months |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F-7 Islamabad | PKR 12,000 | PKR 32,400 | PKR 61,200 | PKR 108,000 |
| Bahria Town Phase 7, Rawalpindi | PKR 12,000 | PKR 32,400 | PKR 61,200 | PKR 108,000 |
| G-11 Islamabad | PKR 8,000 | PKR 21,600 | PKR 40,800 | PKR 72,000 |

G-11 is cheaper because it is our compact branch. It has a single shared floor with 2 power racks and dumbbells up to 40 kg, and it has no pool, steam or sauna. It is also closed on Sundays.

The one-time **admission fee of PKR 10,000** applies to 1-month and 3-month plans. It is waived on 6-month and 12-month prepaid plans.

## What's included
- Gym floor access at your **home branch only**, meaning the branch where you registered your fingerprint
- Free day lockers (bring your own padlock or buy one at the desk for PKR 500)
- **One InBody body-composition scan per quarter** (extra scans cost PKR 800)
- A free 30-minute floor orientation with a trainer when you join
- Female Standard members can use both the main floor and the Ladies Floor (F-7), the Ladies Wing (Bahria) or ladies hours (G-11) at their home branch

## What's not included
- **Group classes.** Standard members can join a class as a drop-in for **PKR 1,000 per class**, if there is space. Book it in the app or on WhatsApp up to 48 hours ahead.
- **Other branches.** Your fingerprint only opens the door at your home branch. To train at all three branches you need Plus or Elite.
- **Pool, steam and sauna.** A pool drop-in at F-7 costs PKR 1,500 per swim.
- Personal training (PT packages from PKR 18,000 for 8 sessions).

## Changing your home branch
Standard members may change their home branch **free of charge once per quarter**. If you move from G-11 (PKR 8,000) to F-7 or Bahria (PKR 12,000), you pay the price difference for the remaining period of your plan. If you move the other way, from F-7 or Bahria to G-11, **no refund of the difference** is given.

## Discounts on Standard
- **Students (16–25):** 25% off, e.g. G-11 becomes PKR 6,000 and F-7 or Bahria becomes PKR 9,000 per month. Entry is limited to off-peak hours, Mon–Fri 6:00am–4:00pm.
- **Seniors (60+):** 20% off, i.e. PKR 9,600 at F-7 or Bahria and PKR 6,400 at G-11, plus a free physio screening.
- **Corporate** (15% or 20%) and **family** (15%) discounts also apply.
- Only one discount can be used at a time.

## Is Standard right for you?
Choose Standard if you train alone near home or work. If you want more than 4 classes a month, Plus is better value.
