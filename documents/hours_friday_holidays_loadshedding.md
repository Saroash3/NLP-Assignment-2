# Friday Prayers, Public Holidays, Eid and Load-shedding

## Friday prayer (Jumu'ah) break
Every Friday, **all Zor Fitness Club branches close 1:00pm–2:15pm** for Jumu'ah prayer. This applies to:
- All main floors, the F-7 Ladies Floor, the Bahria Ladies Wing and G-11 ladies hours
- All studios and the F-7 pool
- The Zor Juice Bar and Zor Café

Members on the floor at 1:00pm are asked to finish their set and leave. The floors reopen at 2:15pm. **No classes are ever scheduled in this slot**, and PT sessions can't be booked between 1:00pm and 2:15pm on Fridays. The separate prayer rooms at F-7 and Bahria, and the prayer area at G-11, stay open for members all day.

## Eid closures
Every branch is **closed on**:
- **Eid-ul-Fitr: day 1 and day 2**
- **Eid-ul-Adha: day 1 and day 2**

Your membership is **automatically extended by the closed days**. You don't need to request anything. For example, a plan ending on the 10th is extended to the 14th if two Eid closures of 2 days each fall inside it. Frozen members also get the days added after their freeze ends.

## Other public holidays
On **14 August (Independence Day)**, **23 March (Pakistan Day)** and other gazetted holidays, all branches open **10:00am–6:00pm**, **on all floors**, including the Ladies Floor, the Ladies Wing, and G-11 for both men and women on a shared schedule announced in the app. Days with shortened hours are **not** added back to memberships. Classes normally don't run on public holidays unless the app says otherwise.

## Load-shedding and power outages
Every branch has **back-up generators and a UPS**, so we **stay open during load-shedding**.
- Lights, treadmills and other powered cardio machines keep running.
- During outages **longer than 2 hours**, the **air-conditioning runs at reduced power** to save fuel. In summer the floor may feel warmer, so bring water and take breaks.
- At F-7 the **lift may stop** during long outages. Use the stairs to the Ladies Floor (2nd floor) and the basement pool.
- The pool's heating (November–March) may pause during very long outages. The pool stays open.
- Classes continue normally, though the spin studio's music may be quieter.

## Weather and emergencies
If the government or district administration announces a lockdown or an emergency closure, all members get the closed days **added to their membership automatically**, just like Eid. Announcements are sent through the Zor Fitness app and WhatsApp.

## Ramadan
Ramadan has its own split timings: a day session and a night session after taraweeh. The pool is closed for the month. See the Ramadan hours document.
