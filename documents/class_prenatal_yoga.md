# Prenatal Yoga

A gentle, safe class for expecting mothers, taught by a certified prenatal instructor on the F-7 Ladies Floor.

## Class details
- **When:** **Saturday, 11:00am–12:00pm** (weekly). It does not run in Ramadan.
- **Where:** F-7 Islamabad, Ladies Floor (2nd floor, College Road entrance)
- **Instructor:** **Mahnoor Rizvi**, a 500-hour yoga teacher and **certified prenatal instructor**
- **Capacity:** 15
- **Level:** suitable for all trimesters, **with a doctor's clearance**

## The doctor's clearance rule
Before your first Prenatal Yoga class you must hand in a **doctor's clearance**: a short note or prescription slip from your gynaecologist confirming that gentle exercise is safe for you. We keep it on file and ask for an updated note **each trimester**. Without clearance, you cannot join, even as a drop-in. You must also declare the pregnancy on your PAR-Q health form.

## What happens in class
- Breathing exercises for relaxation and labour preparation
- Gentle stretches for the back, hips and pelvic floor
- Modified poses using bolsters, blocks and chairs. There is no lying flat on the back after the first trimester, and no deep twists.
- Relaxation at the end, lying on your side
- A small group, so Mahnoor adjusts each pose to your trimester

## Who can join?
- **Ladies Only, Plus and Elite** members: free, booked in the app or on WhatsApp up to 48 hours ahead (Elite 72 hours)
- **Standard** female members whose home branch is F-7, and **non-members**: PKR 1,000 drop-in
- Non-members also fill in the PAR-Q form and bring their CNIC

## Other rules during pregnancy
- **Steam room and sauna are not allowed** during pregnancy, at any branch
- HIIT Blast, Spin and heavy lifting classes are **not recommended**. Speak to your doctor before any high-intensity training.
- Pregnant members on plans of 3 months or longer can use the **medical freeze**: up to **60 days** free with a doctor's certificate, in addition to the normal freeze allowance. Many members use it around delivery.
- The **ladies pool session** (Mon–Sat 11:00am–3:00pm, female lifeguard) is popular for gentle swimming. Please check with your doctor first.

## After delivery
Once your doctor clears you to exercise, usually after the postpartum check-up, **Pilates (Ladies)** at Bahria (Mon/Wed 10:00–11:00am) and **Ladies Stretch & Mobility** at G-11 (Sat 12:00–12:45pm) are good first classes. **Sana Iftikhar** can design a PT programme for rebuilding strength, and physiotherapist **Dr. Ayesha Malik** can assess the pelvic floor and back (PKR 3,500 assessment, 20% off for members).

## Kids' play area
Mothers with older children aged 3–8 can use the free supervised kids' play area on the F-7 Ladies Floor during class, for a maximum of 90 minutes.
