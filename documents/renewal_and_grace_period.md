# Renewal, Grace Period, Upgrades and Rejoining

## No auto-renewal
Zor Fitness Club **never auto-renews** a membership and never charges your card automatically. You renew by paying at any branch front desk or in the Zor Fitness app. The app sends you a **renewal reminder 7 days before** your plan expires, and the front desk may also remind you when you scan in.

## The 5-day grace period
When your plan expires you get a **5-day grace period**:
- Your fingerprint entry keeps working for those 5 days
- You can renew at the normal price with no penalty and no admission fee
- Your new plan starts from the original expiry date, not from the day you pay. Grace days are not free days.

## Inactive status
If you haven't renewed after the 5 grace days, your account becomes **inactive** and entry stops.
- **Renew within 60 days** of expiry: you just pay the plan price. Your new plan starts on the day you pay.
- **Inactive for more than 60 days:** you rejoin as a new member and pay the **PKR 10,000 admission fee** again, unless you take a 6-month or 12-month plan, where it is waived.
- Frozen days never count as inactive time.

## Upgrading in the middle of a plan
You can upgrade at any time, for example Standard → Plus or Plus → Elite. You pay the **pro-rata difference** for the days left on your current plan. For example, a member with exactly half a month left on Plus (PKR 16,000) who upgrades to Elite (PKR 26,000) pays half of the PKR 10,000 difference.

## Downgrading
Downgrades (for example Elite → Plus) are **not possible in the middle of a plan**. They take effect only at your next renewal. No refund is given for the higher plan's remaining days.

## Changing branch or plan type at renewal
At renewal you can switch plan types, move your Standard home branch, or change between monthly and prepaid terms at no extra charge. If you move a Standard membership from G-11 to F-7 or Bahria, the new branch's price applies.

## Renewing with a discount
Student members must show a valid student ID again. It is rechecked every 6 months. Family discounts continue only if at least 3 household members are still active. Couple discounts need both partners to be active on Plus or Elite.

## How to pay
Cash, debit or credit card, bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa. Cheques are not accepted. 12-month renewals can again be split into 2 instalments, with the second due on the first day of month 7.
