# InBody Body-Composition Scans

An InBody scan measures more than your weight. It estimates your **body fat percentage, skeletal muscle mass, body water, and visceral fat**, so you can see whether you are losing fat or losing muscle.

## How to prepare
For accurate, comparable results:
- Scan **in the morning**, before training
- **Don't eat for 2 hours** before the scan, and use the washroom first
- **No exercise or steam or sauna** just before
- Remove shoes, socks, watches and jewellery
- **Don't scan during your period** if you want to compare results month to month, because water retention changes the numbers
- **Not recommended during pregnancy**, and not for people with a pacemaker or other implanted electronic device

## Understanding your results
- **Weight** is split into **fat mass**, **muscle mass** and **water**
- **Body fat %:** typical healthy ranges differ for men and women. Your trainer will explain yours.
- **Visceral fat level:** fat around the organs. Lowering it is a key health goal.
- **Skeletal muscle mass:** should go **up** or stay steady during weight loss if you're doing strength training
- Results are saved in your **Zor Fitness app** history so you can see trends over time

## Who uses the results?
- **Your trainer**, to adjust your programme. For example, Hira Saleem uses InBody to track fat loss rather than just scale weight.
