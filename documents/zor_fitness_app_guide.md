# Zor Fitness App Guide

The **Zor Fitness** app (Android and iOS) is the easiest way to manage your membership. Download it from the Play Store or App Store and log in with your CNIC number and the phone number you registered at the front desk.

## What you can do in the app
### Classes
- **Book classes** up to **48 hours** ahead (**72 hours for Elite**)
- **Cancel** at least **3 hours** before, otherwise it counts as a no-show
- **Join a waitlist** for full classes. If a spot opens you're booked automatically and get a WhatsApp message.
- See your **no-show count** for the month. 3 no-shows means a 7-day booking block, and the count resets on the 1st.
- Standard members pay the **PKR 1,000 drop-in** in the app by card or JazzCash

### Personal training
- Book PT sessions with any trainer at the branches your plan covers
- See how many sessions are left in your package, and its **60-day expiry date**
- Cancel with **12 hours' notice**
- Elite members see their **4 included monthly sessions** and when they expire (end of the month)

### Membership
- **Renew** your plan, with a reminder **7 days before expiry**
- **Upgrade** your plan in the middle of a plan (you pay the pro-rata difference)
- **Request a freeze** (Profile → Freeze). Submit it **48 hours** ahead. Medical freeze certificates can be uploaded here.
- See your **freeze allowance** used and remaining
- View **payment receipts** and instalment due dates

### Health and progress
- **InBody scan history** with charts for fat mass and muscle mass
- Workout logs from your trainer. Hamza Qureshi, Usman Tariq and Ali Raza log sessions here.
- Share notes with nutritionist **Kamran Baig** (only with your permission)

### Other features
- **Referrals** (Profile → Referrals): see friends who joined with your name and the 2-week rewards you earned (maximum 3 per year)
- **Feedback** (Profile → Feedback): suggestions and complaints
- **Notices:** Eid closures, Ramadan timetables, class cancellations, load-shedding updates
- **Emergency contact:** staff use it if something happens to you at the club

## What the app can't do
- It can't open the door. Entry is by **fingerprint** only.
- It can't process refunds. Use the refund form at the desk or email hello@zorfitness.pk.
- It can't transfer a membership. The one-time family transfer of a 12-month plan (PKR 3,000) is done at the front desk with both CNICs.
- It can't book the kids' play area. Sign in at the F-7 Ladies Floor desk.

Forgot your login phone number? Visit the desk with your CNIC. Cash payments can take up to 1 hour to appear in the app.
