# Azadi Offer (Independence Day)

Every August, Zor Fitness Club celebrates Pakistan's Independence Day with the **Azadi Offer** for members who commit to a full year.

## The offer
Buy **any 12-month plan between 1 and 14 August** and get:
- **1 extra month free**, so you get **13 months** for the price of 12
- A **free green Zor tracksuit**, available only with Azadi purchases and while stocks last at each branch
- The normal **12-month price** (25% off the monthly price) and **no admission fee**, as on every 12-month plan

## Azadi prices (13 months for the 12-month price)
| Plan | Price |
|---|---|
| Standard (F-7 / Bahria) | PKR 108,000 |
| Standard (G-11) | PKR 72,000 |
| Plus | PKR 144,000 |
| Elite | PKR 234,000 |
| Ladies Only | PKR 90,000 |

## Rules
- It must be **bought** between **1 and 14 August**. Your **start date** can be any day up to **31 August**.
- It is available to **new and existing members**. Existing members can buy it as their next renewal. The 13 months start when their current plan ends, or on the chosen start date if that is later.
- It **cannot be combined** with the student, corporate, couple, family or senior discounts. If you normally get a discount, choose either your discount or the Azadi Offer.
- The extra month is added at the end of the plan. It is not a separate voucher and cannot be transferred on its own.
- Instalments are allowed: 2 equal instalments, with the second due on the first day of month 7. The normal PKR 1,500 late fee applies after 7 days.
- The freeze allowance is the normal 12-month allowance (30 days). The extra month does not add extra freeze days.
- If a 12-month Azadi plan is transferred to a family member (a one-time transfer for PKR 3,000, with at least 3 months left), the extra month goes with it.

## 14 August opening hours
On **14 August** itself all branches open **10:00am–6:00pm**, on all floors, as on other gazetted holidays. The Azadi Offer can be bought at the desk that day. The 14th is the **last day** of the offer.

## Why 12 months?
A year-long plan gets the biggest prepaid discount (25%), no admission fee, the 30-day freeze allowance, relocation refund eligibility, and the one-time family transfer option. The Azadi month on top makes it the best-value time of year to join.
