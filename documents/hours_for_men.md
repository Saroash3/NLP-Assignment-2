# Gym Timings for Men: When Can Males Train?

Zor Fitness Club is a mixed gym, and men can train at all three branches. Men use the **main floor** at F-7 and Bahria, and the **men's hours** of the shared floor at G-11. These are the regular timings (see the end for Ramadan and holidays).

## Men's timings at F-7 Islamabad
- **Main floor:** Monday to Saturday 6:00am–11:00pm, Sunday 8:00am–8:00pm.
- **Men's pool sessions:** Monday to Saturday 6:00–9:00am and 7:00–10:00pm (pool included on Elite; PKR 1,500 per swim on other plans).
- **Men's steam and sauna**, and a men's prayer room on the ground floor.

## Men's timings at Bahria Town Phase 7, Rawalpindi
- **Main floor:** Monday to Saturday 6:00am–12:00am (midnight), Sunday 10:00am–8:00pm.
- Men's steam room (no sauna), spin studio, boxing zone and prayer room.
- Bahria has the latest closing time of all branches: midnight, Monday to Saturday.

## Men's timings at G-11 Islamabad
- G-11 has **one shared floor**, so men have **two separate blocks**, Monday to Saturday: a **morning block 6:00am to 10:00am** and an **evening block 5:00pm to 11:00pm**. It is not open to men all day.
- Between the two blocks (10:00am to 5:00pm) men cannot train at G-11: that time is for women and for cleaning the floor.
- G-11 is **closed on Sundays**, and it has no pool and no steam or sauna.

## Rules that apply to everyone
- **Last entry** is 45 minutes before closing.
- **Friday prayer break:** all gym floors, studios and the pool close 1:00pm–2:15pm every Friday for Jumu'ah.

## Ramadan and holidays
- **Ramadan (men / main floor, all branches):** 10:00am–3:00pm and 9:30pm–2:00am. The pool is closed during Ramadan, and steam and sauna open in the night session only.
- **Eid:** closed on Eid-ul-Fitr days 1–2 and Eid-ul-Adha days 1–2.
- **14 August, 23 March and other gazetted holidays:** open 10:00am–6:00pm.

## Which plan for men?
Men can join Standard, Plus or Elite. Standard covers one home branch; Plus and Elite cover all three branches.
