## Rules that apply everywhere
- **Last entry is 45 minutes before closing.** For example, the F-7 main floor's last entry on weekdays is 10:15pm.
- **Steam and sauna close 30 minutes before** the floor closes.
- **Friday prayer break:** every branch, floor, studio and the pool closes **1:00pm–2:15pm** on Fridays for Jumu'ah.
- **Student members** (25% discount) can only enter **Mon–Fri 6:00am–4:00pm**.

## Where can women train on Sunday?
Only at the **F-7 Ladies Floor, 10:00am–6:00pm**. The Bahria Ladies Wing and G-11 are both closed on Sundays. Female members on Standard with G-11 or Bahria as their home branch can't train on Sunday unless they change home branch or upgrade to Plus, Elite or Ladies Only. Women on Standard, Plus or Elite can also use the main floors on Sunday at F-7 and Bahria.

## Latest and earliest options
- **Earliest:** 6:00am on the F-7 and Bahria main floors and G-11 men's hours (Mon–Sat)
- **Latest:** midnight at the Bahria main floor (Mon–Sat)
- **Latest for women:** 10:00pm on the F-7 Ladies Floor (Mon–Sat)
