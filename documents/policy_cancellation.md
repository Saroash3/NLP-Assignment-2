# Cancellation Policy: Memberships, Classes and PT

Three kinds of cancellation at Zor Fitness Club.

## 1. Cancelling a membership
### Monthly plans
There is **no contract** on monthly plans and **no auto-renewal**, so there is nothing to "cancel". Simply **don't renew** when your plan ends. We never charge your card automatically.
- After expiry you get a **5-day grace period**, then your account becomes inactive
- If you rejoin within **60 days**, you just pay the plan price
- After more than 60 days inactive, the **PKR 10,000 admission fee** applies again, unless you take a 6 or 12-month plan

### Prepaid plans (3, 6, 12 months)
Prepaid plans **cannot be cancelled for a refund** except under the refund policy:
1. The **7-day cooling-off**, with no visits at all (biometric log checked). The plan fee is refunded but not the admission fee.
2. **Relocation** more than 50 km from all branches (6 and 12-month plans only): unused full months minus 10%
3. **Permanent medical inability** (6 and 12-month plans only): the same calculation as relocation

If you can't use your plan for a while, consider a **freeze** instead: 7, 15 or 30 days depending on term, plus up to 60 days for medical reasons. On a 12-month plan, a **one-time family transfer** (PKR 3,000) is also possible if at least 3 months remain.

## 2. Cancelling a class booking: 3 hours' notice
- Cancel at least **3 hours before** the class in the app or on WhatsApp
- Later cancellations or not showing up count as a **no-show**
- **3 no-shows in a calendar month = a 7-day class-booking block**
- Arriving **more than 10 minutes late** also counts as a no-show, because the studio door closes
- Drop-in fees (PKR 1,000) are not refunded for late cancellations

## 3. Cancelling a personal training session: 12 hours' notice
- Cancel at least **12 hours before** your PT session
- Later cancellations or no-shows mean the **session is forfeited** (counted as used)
- If your **trainer** is more than 10 minutes late, you get the session **free**
- PT packages expire **60 days** after purchase, and cancelled sessions don't extend this

## When Zor cancels
- **Classes** with fewer than 3 bookings 2 hours before the start may be cancelled. You get a WhatsApp message, it never counts as a no-show, and drop-ins are credited.
- **Terminated memberships:** Zor may end a membership immediately **without refund** for harassment, a second tailgating offence, coaching other members for money, or selling supplements on the premises.
