## Quick facts
- **Busiest day for classes:** Tuesday and Thursday evenings at Bahria (HIIT Blast, then Spin Night)
- **Only Sunday class:** Sunday Long Ride
- **Earliest class:** Spin at 6:30am (Mon/Wed/Fri)
- **Latest class:** Boxing Conditioning, ending at 9:00pm (Tue/Thu)
- Student members (off-peak, Mon–Fri 6:00am–4:00pm) can attend the morning and daytime classes only
- In Ramadan a separate night-only timetable replaces this one
