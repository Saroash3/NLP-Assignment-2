# Guest Pass Policy

Members may bring guests to Zor Fitness Club under the following rules.

## Price
- **PKR 1,000 per guest visit**, paid at the front desk
- **Elite members** get **2 free guest passes per month**. They don't roll over to the next month.

## Guest requirements
- The guest must be **16 or older**
- The guest shows their **original CNIC**. Guests aged 16–17 show their **B-form** plus a **parent's written consent**.
- The guest signs a **health declaration form** before entering
- Guests follow the same dress code: closed sports shoes, a towel, no jeans or chappals
- The **member must stay with their guest at all times** and is **responsible for their guest's behaviour**. If a guest breaks the rules, the member can receive a warning.

## Limits
- **Maximum 4 guest visits per member per month**, including Elite's free passes
- **The same guest can come at most 2 times per month**, with any member. Someone who wants to train regularly should take a day pass (PKR 1,500) or join.
- **Free-trial rule:** a person who has used their **free trial day** can't come as a guest until **7 days** after the trial

## Where guests can and can't go
- Guests can use the **gym floor** only
- Guests **cannot** use the **pool**, **steam room** or **sauna**
- Guests **cannot** join group classes on a guest pass. They can book a drop-in class for PKR 1,000 instead.
- **Ladies Floor (F-7), Ladies Wing (Bahria) and G-11 ladies hours:** guests **must be female**
- Standard members can bring guests to their **home branch** only
- Guests can't use the kids' play area for their children

## The G-11 peak-time rule
**No guests are allowed at G-11 Monday to Friday, 6:00–9:00pm.** G-11 has a single compact floor with only 2 power racks, and weekday evenings are too crowded. Guests are welcome at G-11 at other times.

## When guests are not allowed at all
- **Frozen members** can't bring guests and can't enter as guests themselves
- **Suspended members** (unpaid instalment) can't bring guests
- Student members can bring guests only during their off-peak window (Mon–Fri 6:00am–4:00pm)

## Guest pass vs day pass
| | Guest pass | Day pass |
|---|---|---|
| Price | PKR 1,000 | PKR 1,500 |
| Needs a member present | Yes, all the time | No |
| Limit | 2 per month for the same guest | None |
| Branches | Member's allowed branches | Any branch |

## Examples
- An Elite member brings her sister twice in March: both visits use the free passes, costing PKR 0.
- A Plus member brings the same friend a third time in one month: not allowed. The friend should buy a day pass.
- A Standard member at G-11 wants to bring a colleague at 7:00pm on a Tuesday: not allowed, because of the peak-time rule. Come on Saturday evening instead.
