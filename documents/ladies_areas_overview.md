# Women's Facilities at Zor Fitness Club

Every Zor branch has a women-only option, with female trainers and female staff. Here is how it works at each branch.

## F-7 Islamabad: the Ladies Floor
- The **entire 2nd floor**, with its **own side entrance on College Road**, so women don't pass through the main floor
- Open **Mon–Sat 7:00am–10:00pm, Sunday 10:00am–6:00pm**. It is the only women's area open on Sundays.
- Full cardio and weights equipment, a class studio, the **ladies steam room and sauna**, and a ladies prayer room
- The **kids' play area** for children aged 3–8, free for any female member's children while she trains here. It is supervised 10:00am–4:00pm, for a maximum of 90 minutes.
- The **ladies pool session** in the basement pool, Mon–Sat 11:00am–3:00pm, with a **female lifeguard**
- Classes: Ladies Strength & PCOS Program, Yoga Flow, Prenatal Yoga and Zumba

## Bahria Town Phase 7: the Ladies Wing
- A ground-floor wing with its **own rear entrance**
- Open **Mon–Sat 8:00am–9:00pm, closed Sunday**
- Full equipment, a studio, and the **ladies steam room** (there is no sauna at Bahria)
- Classes: Pilates (Mon/Wed 10:00–11:00am) and Zumba (Tue/Thu 4:00–5:00pm)

## G-11 Islamabad: ladies hours
- The single shared floor is **women-only Mon–Sat 10:30am–4:30pm**. It is cleaned before and after (10:00–10:30am and 4:30–5:00pm).
- Female staff on duty during ladies hours
- Classes: Ladies Zor Circuit (Tue/Thu 11:00–11:45am) and Ladies Stretch & Mobility (Sat 12:00–12:45pm) with Hira Saleem
- No steam, sauna or pool at G-11. Closed on Sundays.

## Female trainers
**Sana Iftikhar** (strength and PCOS, F-7), **Mahnoor Rizvi** (yoga, Pilates, prenatal), **Farah Naz** (Zumba), **Hira Saleem** (functional training and weight loss, G-11). Physiotherapist **Dr. Ayesha Malik** is also female. Only female trainers coach on the Ladies Floor and Ladies Wing.

## Which plans give access?
- **Ladies Only (PKR 10,000/month):** women's areas at all 3 branches, all ladies classes, ladies steam and sauna, and the ladies pool session plus family swim
- **Standard, Plus, Elite:** female members can use **both** the main floor and the ladies areas at the branches their plan covers

## Privacy rules
- **No photography or filming** by anyone on the Ladies Floor, in the Ladies Wing or in changing rooms, not even of yourself
- Guests must be female
- **Dress:** any athletic wear. Sports hijab and full-length athletic wear are welcome. Closed sports shoes are required.
- Biometric entry means only registered female members can open the ladies entrances.
