# Sehri and Iftar Tips for Training in Ramadan

*General guidance, not medical advice. People with diabetes, blood pressure problems, pregnancy or other conditions should ask their doctor about fasting and exercise. For a personal Ramadan meal plan, book nutritionist Kamran Baig (PKR 4,000, or included once a month on Elite).*

## The main idea
When you fast, you have a shorter eating window, so **what** you eat at sehri and iftar matters more. The goal is steady energy, enough protein, and enough water, without the heavy fried-food crash.

## Sehri: eat slow-digesting food
- **Protein:** eggs, dahi, or leftover chicken or daal
- **Slow carbs:** a whole-wheat roti, or oats (daliya) with milk
- **Fibre and water-rich food:** fruit such as bananas or apples, and cucumber
- **Water:** drink steadily from iftar to sehri, rather than gulping a lot at sehri
- **Avoid:** very salty food, which makes you thirsty the next day, and lots of paratha with extra ghee, sugary drinks and too much chai, since caffeine can increase thirst

## Iftar: break the fast gently
1. Start with **dates and water**. It is traditional and gives quick energy.
2. Have a **light soup or fruit chaat** with less chaat masala and sugar.
3. **Pray Maghrib**, then eat a balanced meal: salan, roti or rice, and salad.
4. **Limit pakoras, samosas and jalebi.** Enjoy one or two, not a plateful every day.
5. **Rooh Afza and sweet drinks:** have a small glass, not a jug.

## When to train in Ramadan
Zor's Ramadan hours:
- **Men / main floor:** 10:00am–3:00pm (day) and 9:30pm–2:00am (night)
- **Ladies:** 11:00am–3:00pm (day) and 9:00pm–12:00am (night)

Our recommendation:
- **Day session (while fasting):** light training only. Walking, stretching, mobility and technique practice, for less than 45 minutes. Steam and sauna are not allowed while fasting.
- **Night session (after taraweeh):** the best time for strength training and classes. You've eaten and can drink water. Have a light snack (a banana-date shake or boiled eggs from the Café) if iftar was early.
- The pool is closed in Ramadan.

## Ramadan night classes
Zor Circuit (G-11), HIIT Blast and Spin Night (Bahria), Ladies Yoga Flow (F-7) and Ladies Zumba (Bahria Ladies Wing). Keep intensity moderate in the first week while your body adjusts.

## Warning signs: stop training if you feel
- Dizziness or blurred vision
- A pounding headache or confusion
- Chest pain or unusual breathlessness
- Muscle cramps that won't stop

Tell a staff member immediately. All staff are first-aid trained, and every branch has an AED.

## Eid after Ramadan
After a month of fasting, don't go straight back to your heaviest weights. Normal classes restart on the first working day after the Eid-ul-Fitr closure. Build back up over 1–2 weeks.
