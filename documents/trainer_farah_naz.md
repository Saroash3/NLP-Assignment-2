# Trainer Profile: Farah Naz

**Role:** Zumba and Dance Fitness Instructor
**Branches:** F-7 Ladies Floor (Mon/Wed) and Bahria Ladies Wing (Tue/Thu)
**Qualifications:** Licensed Zumba instructor since 2018
**Languages:** Urdu, English, Punjabi

## About Farah
Farah Naz is the energy behind Zor Fitness Club's most-booked ladies classes. She has been a **licensed Zumba instructor since 2018** and joined Zor when the F-7 Ladies Floor opened. Her classes mix Latin rhythms with Bollywood and Punjabi tracks, and many members say they forget they're exercising. Farah keeps her choreography simple and repeats it for several weeks so new members can follow along quickly. No dance experience is needed.

## Personal sessions
Farah offers private or small-group dance fitness sessions from **2:00 to 4:00pm on her branch days**:
- F-7 Ladies Floor: Monday and Wednesday
- Bahria Ladies Wing: Tuesday and Thursday

These are useful for brides-to-be, friends booking together, or members who want to learn the routines before joining a full class. PT package prices are the same as for all trainers (8 sessions PKR 18,000, 12 sessions PKR 25,000, 20 sessions PKR 38,000). **Farah does not offer weightlifting PT.** For strength training, she refers members to Sana Iftikhar (F-7) or Hira Saleem (G-11).

## What a Farah class looks like
- A 5-minute warm-up with easy steps
- 45 minutes of songs, alternating fast and slow tracks, with options for high or low impact
- A 10-minute cool-down and stretch
- Burns a lot of energy while feeling like a party. Bring a towel (mandatory) and a full water bottle.

## Who should join?
- Women who get bored with treadmills
- Beginners, because every move has an easy version
- Members wanting a social, fun workout with friends
- Anyone preparing for a wedding season. Farah runs special mehndi-dance routines in the weeks before winter wedding season.

## Rules
- Women only; any female member with access (Ladies Only, Plus, Elite; Standard members pay a PKR 1,000 drop-in)
- **Closed sports shoes are required**, and no bare feet in Zumba
- No filming on the Ladies Floor or in the Ladies Wing, even of the choreography
- Cancel at least 3 hours before, or it counts as a no-show. 3 no-shows in a month means a 7-day booking block.
