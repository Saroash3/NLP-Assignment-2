# Sleep, Recovery and Muscle Soreness

*General guidance from the Zor Fitness Club coaching team. It is not medical advice. For injuries, book physiotherapist Dr. Ayesha Malik or see your doctor.*

Training breaks your muscles down. **Recovery** is when they rebuild stronger. Many members train hard but recover badly, and then stop progressing.

## Sleep: the most underrated recovery tool
- Aim for **7–8 hours** a night. Ali Raza tells every muscle-gain client this first.
- Keep a regular bedtime, even on weekends
- Put your phone away 30 minutes before sleeping
- Avoid heavy dinners and strong chai or coffee late at night
- Late-night trainers at Bahria (open until midnight Mon–Sat) should leave enough time to wind down after training

## Rest days
- Beginners: at least **1 rest day between full-body sessions**
- Don't train the **same muscle group two days in a row** with heavy weights
- An "active rest day" (walking, light stretching, the Ladies Stretch & Mobility class at G-11 on Saturday 12:00–12:45pm, or an easy swim at F-7) helps blood flow without adding fatigue

## Muscle soreness (DOMS)
Feeling stiff and sore **1–2 days** after a new or harder workout is normal. It is called delayed-onset muscle soreness.
- **What helps:** light movement, walking, gentle stretching, good sleep, enough protein and water
- **What doesn't help much:** doing nothing at all, or training the same sore muscle hard again
- It usually fades within 2–3 days

## When soreness is NOT normal
Stop and get it checked if you have:
- **Sharp** or stabbing pain during a lift
- Pain in a **joint** (knee, shoulder, lower back) rather than in the muscle
- Swelling, bruising, or pain that gets worse after 3 days
- Numbness or tingling

## Steam, sauna and pool for recovery
- **Steam or sauna** (Plus, Elite and Ladies Only; F-7 has both, and Bahria has steam only) can feel relaxing after training. Maximum **15 minutes**, drink water, and not during pregnancy or while fasting.
- **Swimming** at the F-7 pool is a gentle recovery activity. Remember the house rule: no swimming within 30 minutes after steam or sauna.

## Nutrition for recovery
Protein at every meal, and a proper meal after training. See our pre- and post-workout food guide, or book nutritionist Kamran Baig for a personal plan.

## Medical freeze
If an injury or illness keeps you out, members on **3-month or longer** plans can take a **medical freeze of up to 60 days**, free, with a doctor's certificate. It is on top of the normal freeze allowance.

## Listen to your body
Tired all the time, sleeping badly, and losing strength? You may be overtraining. Take a lighter week and speak to your trainer.
