# Zor Juice Bar (F-7) and Zor Café (Bahria)

## Locations
- **Zor Juice Bar:** F-7 Islamabad, main lobby. There is also a pick-up counter for the Ladies Floor.
- **Zor Café:** Bahria Town Phase 7, next to reception
- **G-11 has no café.** Water dispensers only.

The menu also includes seasonal fresh juices, and dates and fruit bowls after iftar in Ramadan. Prices may change seasonally.

## Member discount
**Members get 10% off** at both the Juice Bar and the Café. Show your member card or the app. Day-pass holders and guests pay full price.

## Payment
**Card or JazzCash only**, with **no cash** at the Juice Bar or Café. Cash payments are accepted only at the front desk, for memberships.

## Hours
- It follows the branch's main-floor hours
- **Closed on Fridays 1:00pm–2:15pm** for Jumu'ah
- **Ramadan:** open in the **night session only**, after iftar
- Closed on Eid days 1–2

## Rules
- **Food and drinks are consumed at the Juice Bar or Café area only.** There is no food on the gym floor, in the studios or on the pool deck.
- Shakes can be taken to the changing room in a closed bottle
- **Don't mix your own protein powder at the water dispensers.** It clogs the taps. You can mix it at the changing-room sink, or buy a shake.
- **Selling supplements on the premises is prohibited.** The Juice Bar makes shakes but does not sell tubs of protein or pre-workout. Members are also not allowed to sell supplements to each other.

## Why these items?
Our nutritionist **Kamran Baig** helped design the menu around simple, local, high-protein options:
- **Boiled eggs and chicken wraps** for affordable protein after training
- **Banana-date shake** as a natural pre-workout energy drink, popular in Ramadan after iftar
- **Shikanjabeen** for hydration in summer. Ask for less sugar.
- **Black coffee** without sugar as a light pre-workout option

For a full personal meal plan, book a consultation with Kamran (PKR 4,000, or included once a month on Elite).

## Water
All branches have water dispensers on every floor. **Bring your own bottle**, because there are **no plastic cups**. This is part of our effort to reduce plastic waste.

## Corporate orders
Companies on the corporate scheme can pre-order shakes for group sessions. Ask at the F-7 or Bahria front desk.
