# How to Join Zor Fitness Club

Joining takes about 20 minutes at any branch front desk: F-7 Islamabad, G-11 Islamabad or Bahria Town Phase 7 Rawalpindi.

## Step 1: Try it first (optional)
First-time visitors get **one free trial day** and a free InBody scan. It's one per CNIC. Book on WhatsApp (0333-5550967) or in the Zor Fitness app, or walk in. Last entry is 45 minutes before closing.

## Step 2: Choose your plan
| Plan | Monthly price (PKR) |
|---|---|
| Standard | 12,000 at F-7 and Bahria, 8,000 at G-11 (home branch only) |
| Plus | 16,000 (all branches and all classes) |
| Elite | 26,000 (includes PT, nutrition, pool and guest passes) |
| Ladies Only | 10,000 (women's areas at all branches) |

Prepaying saves 10% (3 months), 15% (6 months) or 25% (12 months). Tell the desk if you are a student, a senior aged 60+, joining as a couple or family, or joining through your employer. **Only one discount can apply.**

## Step 3: Bring these documents
- **Original CNIC.** Members aged **16–17** bring their **B-form**, and a parent or guardian must come in person to sign consent.
- One passport-size photo, or we take a photo at the desk
- For discounts: a student ID (16–25), a nikahnama or family registration certificate (couples), or your company letter or HR list (corporate)
- Children aged 13–15 can only join supervised group classes, with a parent's signed consent

## Step 4: Health declaration (PAR-Q)
Everyone fills in the PAR-Q health form. Please declare heart conditions, high blood pressure, diabetes, asthma, recent surgery, joint replacements or pregnancy. Members with a heart condition need a **doctor's note before joining HIIT Blast or Spin**. Prenatal Yoga needs a doctor's clearance.

## Step 5: Pay
Pay your plan price plus the **one-time PKR 10,000 admission fee**. It is waived on 6-month and 12-month plans, for corporate members, and with the Ramadan Fit Pass. Pay by cash, card (no surcharge), bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa. **Cheques are not accepted.** 12-month plans can be split into 2 instalments.

## Step 6: Fingerprint and card
Your fingerprint is registered for biometric entry at the branches your plan covers. You also get a member card, which costs PKR 1,000 to replace if lost. Never let anyone in on your fingerprint: tailgating gets a written warning the first time and termination the second time.

## Step 7: Orientation
Book your **free 30-minute floor orientation** with a trainer. You will learn the machines, the floor rules (a towel is mandatory, and closed sports shoes, no jeans) and how to book classes in the app. Your first InBody scan is included.

## Cooling-off
If you change your mind within **7 days** and haven't visited even once (we check the fingerprint log), you get a full refund of the plan fee. The PKR 10,000 admission fee is not refunded.
