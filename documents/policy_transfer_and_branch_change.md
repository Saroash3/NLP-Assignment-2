# Membership Transfer and Home-Branch Change Policy

## Memberships are personal
A Zor Fitness Club membership belongs to **one person**, linked to their CNIC and fingerprint. It **cannot be sold, shared, lent or transferred**. There is one exception, described below.

### The tailgating rule
Letting someone enter on your fingerprint or card, or holding the door for a non-member, is called **tailgating**:
- **First time:** a written warning
- **Second time:** **termination of your membership without refund**

## The one exception: family transfer of a 12-month plan
A **12-month plan** (any plan type) can be transferred **once** to an **immediate family member**:
- Allowed family: **spouse, parent, child, or sibling**. Cousins, in-laws and friends are **not** allowed.
- **Transfer fee: PKR 3,000**
- At least **3 months must remain** on the plan at the time of transfer
- Proof of relationship: CNICs, a family registration certificate, or a nikahnama
- The new member fills in the PAR-Q form, registers their fingerprint, and gets a free orientation
- The new member does **not** pay an admission fee
- Remaining freeze days and any Azadi Offer bonus month **move with the plan**. Referral weeks already added also stay with the plan.
- A transferred plan **cannot be transferred again**
- Plans under **instalment suspension** can't be transferred until the balance is paid
- If the plan was bought with a personal discount (e.g. student), the new holder must also qualify, or pay the difference. For example, a student plan transferred to a 30-year-old sibling means paying the difference to the normal price.

3-month and 6-month plans can **never** be transferred. Use a freeze or, if you qualify, the refund policy instead.

## Home-branch change (Standard plan)
Standard members can only enter their **home branch**. To move:
- **Free once per quarter** (every 3 months), requested at the desk or in the app
- **G-11 → F-7 or Bahria:** you pay the **price difference** for the remaining period, because G-11 is PKR 8,000 and F-7 and Bahria are PKR 12,000 per month
- **F-7 or Bahria → G-11:** allowed, but **no refund** of the difference
- **F-7 ↔ Bahria:** same price, no charge
- A second change in the same quarter is not allowed. If you train at several branches, consider **Plus** (PKR 16,000, all branches).

## PT sessions
PT sessions can be moved **between Zor trainers** (for example from Usman Tariq to Ali Raza), but **not to another member**.

## Plan changes
- **Upgrades** in the middle of a plan: pay the pro-rata difference
- **Downgrades:** only at renewal
- **Corporate** memberships belong to the employee while employed. When they leave the company, they can continue as normal members, with no admission fee, at the normal price.

For any transfer, visit the front desk with both people's CNICs, or email hello@zorfitness.pk.
