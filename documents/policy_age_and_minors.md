# Age Policy: Teenagers, Children and Seniors

## Minimum ages
| Age | What is allowed |
|---|---|
| **Under 3** | Family swim only (with a parent in the water) |
| **3–8** | The F-7 kids' play area (children of female members training on the Ladies Floor), and family swim |
| **9–12** | Family swim only |
| **13–15** | **Supervised group classes only**, with a parent's signed consent. No gym floor access. |
| **16–17** | Full gym floor access with a **parent's consent** and a **B-form** |
| **18+** | Full membership |
| **60+** | Full membership, plus the senior discount (20% off Standard) and a free physio screening |

## Teenagers aged 16–17
- Join with their **B-form** (not a CNIC)
- A **parent or guardian must come in person** to sign the consent form
- They can use the gym floor, classes, steam and sauna. Steam and sauna are for 16+ only.
- They can use the **student discount** (ages 16–25) with a valid school or college ID, with off-peak entry only (Mon–Fri 6:00am–4:00pm)
- Guests aged 16–17 also need a B-form and a parent's written consent

## Children aged 13–15
- May join **supervised group classes only**. Examples: Zor Circuit, Ladies Zor Circuit, Yoga Flow, Zumba and Spin, depending on gender and branch.
- A **parent's signed consent** is required
- They are **not** allowed on the gym floor, even with a parent, because weights and machines are not safe for them unsupervised
- They pay a PKR 1,000 drop-in per class, or can hold a Plus membership (classes included) under the family discount
- **Not allowed** in the steam room or sauna

## Kids' play area (F-7 only)
- Free for the children aged **3–8** of **any female member** (on any plan) **while she trains on the F-7 Ladies Floor**
- Supervised **10:00am–4:00pm**, for a **maximum of 90 minutes** per visit
- The mother **must stay in the building** throughout
- It is not available to male members, guests or day-pass holders. There is no kids' area at G-11 or Bahria.

## Family swim (F-7 pool)
- **Sunday 10:00am–2:00pm**, all ages
- **Children under 12 must be in the water with a parent**
- Pool access: included for Elite members, and for Ladies Only members' family swim. Otherwise it is PKR 1,500 per swim.
- A swim cap is mandatory for everyone, including children

## Seniors (60+)
Seniors are very welcome. See the senior discount document. A doctor's note may be requested for heart conditions, and one is required before HIIT Blast or Spin for anyone with a heart condition.

## Why these rules?
The rules protect young people's growing joints and keep everyone safe around heavy equipment. They follow common practice at gyms in Pakistan, where 16 is the usual minimum age for the gym floor.
