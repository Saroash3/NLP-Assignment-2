# Functional Training Classes at G-11: Zor Circuit, Ladies Zor Circuit, Stretch & Mobility

Our compact G-11 branch runs Zor Fitness Club's signature functional classes in its single studio.

## Zor Circuit (men's hours)
- **When:** Monday, Wednesday, Friday, **7:00–7:45am**
- **Coach:** **Usman Tariq**, a kettlebell specialist with 6 years of experience
- **Level:** all levels
- **Capacity:** 15
- **Format:** 45 minutes of timed stations with kettlebell swings, goblet squats, farmer's carries, sled pushes, box step-ups and core work. You rotate every 45 seconds. Beginners use lighter bells, and Usman shows a scaled version of every move.
- **Ramadan:** it is the only G-11 class in Ramadan, moved to Mon/Wed/Fri **10:00–10:45pm**.
- **Good for:** office-goers who want a full-body session before work, and students, because it is inside the off-peak window of Mon–Fri 6:00am–4:00pm.

## Ladies Zor Circuit (ladies hours)
- **When:** Tuesday and Thursday, **11:00–11:45am**
- **Coach:** **Hira Saleem**, a certified personal trainer who specialises in women's weight loss
- **Level:** all levels
- **Capacity:** 15
- **Format:** the same station-based circuit as Zor Circuit, adapted with more lower-body and core focus. It is a popular choice for mothers after the school drop-off, and for students between lectures.
- Women only, during G-11 ladies hours (10:30am–4:30pm), with female staff on duty.

## Ladies Stretch & Mobility
- **When:** Saturday, **12:00–12:45pm**
- **Coach:** Hira Saleem
- **Level:** all levels. It is gentle and suitable for seniors and beginners.
- **Format:** 45 minutes of foam rolling, hip and shoulder mobility, and long-hold stretches. It is a good recovery session after a week of training, and it helps with desk-job stiffness.

## Who can join?
- **Plus and Elite:** all three (women for the ladies classes), free
- **Ladies Only:** Ladies Zor Circuit and Ladies Stretch & Mobility, free
- **Standard members with G-11 as home branch:** PKR 1,000 drop-in, since classes aren't included in Standard. Standard members of other branches can't enter G-11.
- Children aged 13–15 can join with a parent's signed consent

## What to bring
Athletic wear, closed sports shoes and a towel (mandatory). Shalwar kameez isn't allowed in the circuit classes because of the kettlebells and loose fabric. It's only allowed in yoga and Pilates-type studio classes. Bring a water bottle.

## G-11 reminders
- G-11 is **closed on Sundays**
- **No guests at G-11 Mon–Fri 6:00–9:00pm**
- The floor is cleaned 10:00–10:30am and 4:30–5:00pm, between the men's and ladies' sessions
- Street parking only, so arrive a few minutes early for the 7:00am class. The door closes 10 minutes after the start.
