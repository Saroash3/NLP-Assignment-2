# FAQ: Women Members

**Q: Do you have a separate ladies gym?**
A: Yes, at every branch. **F-7:** the whole **2nd-floor Ladies Floor** with its own entrance on College Road. **Bahria:** a separate **Ladies Wing** with a rear entrance. **G-11:** the floor is **women-only 10:30am–4:30pm**, Mon–Sat.

**Q: What are the ladies timings?**
A: **F-7 Ladies Floor:** Mon–Sat 7:00am–10:00pm, Sunday 10:00am–6:00pm. **Bahria Ladies Wing:** Mon–Sat 8:00am–9:00pm, closed Sunday. **G-11:** Mon–Sat 10:30am–4:30pm, closed Sunday.

**Q: Where can I train on Sunday?**
A: Only the **F-7 Ladies Floor (10:00am–6:00pm)** is open for women-only training on Sunday. Female members on Standard, Plus or Elite can also use the main floors at F-7 and Bahria on Sunday.

**Q: Are the trainers female?**
A: Yes. Only female trainers coach on the Ladies Floor and Ladies Wing: **Sana Iftikhar** (strength and PCOS), **Mahnoor Rizvi** (yoga, Pilates, prenatal), **Farah Naz** (Zumba) and **Hira Saleem** (G-11 functional training and weight loss). Physiotherapist **Dr. Ayesha Malik** is also female.

**Q: How much is the Ladies Only plan?**
A: **PKR 10,000 per month**, covering women's areas at all 3 branches, all ladies classes, ladies steam and sauna, and the ladies pool session at F-7.

**Q: Is there a ladies swimming time?**
A: Yes. The F-7 pool is **ladies-only Mon–Sat 11:00am–3:00pm**, with a **female lifeguard**. Burkinis are welcome, and a swim cap is mandatory.

**Q: Can I bring my kids?**
A: Children aged **3–8** can use the free **kids' play area on the F-7 Ladies Floor** while you train there. It is for **any female member on any plan**, supervised 10:00am–4:00pm, for a maximum of 90 minutes, and you must stay in the building. There is no kids' area at G-11 or Bahria.

**Q: Can I wear hijab or abaya?**
A: **Sports hijab and full-length athletic wear are welcome.** Please remove an abaya before using treadmills or cable machines, and use the changing-room hooks.

**Q: Can I take photos for Instagram on the Ladies Floor?**
A: No. **No photography or filming at all** on the Ladies Floor, in the Ladies Wing or in changing rooms, **not even of yourself**.

**Q: Can I bring my friend?**
A: Yes, as a guest for **PKR 1,000**. Guests on the ladies floors must be **female**, and the same guest can come at most twice a month.

**Q: I have PCOS. Which class should I take?**
A: The **Ladies Strength & PCOS Program** with Sana Iftikhar, **Mon/Wed/Fri 11:00am–12:00pm** at F-7. Many members also book nutritionist Kamran Baig. It's fitness support, not medical treatment.

**Q: I'm pregnant. Can I still train?**
A: With your doctor's approval, yes. **Prenatal Yoga** runs **Saturday 11:00am–12:00pm** at F-7 and needs a doctor's clearance. Steam and sauna are not allowed during pregnancy.
