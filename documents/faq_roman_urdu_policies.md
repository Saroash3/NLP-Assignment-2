# FAQ (Roman Urdu): Freeze, Refund aur Qawaid

**Sawal: Kya membership freeze ho sakti hai?**
Jawab: **Monthly plan freeze nahi hota.** 3 mahine ke plan par **7 din**, 6 mahine par **15 din**, aur 12 mahine par **30 din** freeze allowed hai. Kam az kam **7 din** ka freeze, aur aik plan mein zyada se zyada **2 dafa**. Request **48 ghante pehle** karni hoti hai. Peeche ki tareekh (backdate) se freeze nahi hota.

**Sawal: Bimaar ho gaya hoon, kya karoon?**
Jawab: 3 mahine ya us se lambe plan par **doctor ke certificate** ke saath **60 din tak free medical freeze** mil sakta hai. Yeh aam freeze allowance ke ilawa hai.

**Sawal: Paise wapas mil sakte hain?**
Jawab: Aam taur par fees **non-refundable** hai. Sirf 3 surtein hain:
1. **7 din ke andar**, agar aap **aik dafa bhi gym nahi aaye** (fingerprint record check hota hai), to plan fee wapas. **Admission fee wapas nahi hoti.**
2. **Shehar chhor rahe hain** (har branch se **50 km se zyada** door): sirf 6 ya 12 mahine ke plan par, bache hue poore mahinon ka paisa, minus **10% processing fee**.
3. **Mustaqil medical majboori** (doctor certificate), 6 ya 12 mahine ke plan par.
Refund **14 working days** mein milta hai.

**Sawal: Membership apne bhai ko de sakta hoon?**
Jawab: Sirf **12 mahine** ka plan, **aik dafa**, qareebi family (biwi/shohar, walidain, bachay, behan bhai) ko **PKR 3,000** fee par transfer ho sakta hai. Kam az kam **3 mahine** baqi hone chahiyein.

**Sawal: Gym mein kya pehen sakte hain?**
Jawab: Sports kapray aur **band sports joote** zaroori hain. **Jeans, chappal, khussa mana hai.** Gym floor par **shalwar kameez allowed nahi** (machines mein phans sakti hai), lekin yoga aur Pilates class mein theek hai. **Towel zaroori hai**. Bhool jayen to PKR 300 mein kiraye par milta hai. Mardon ke liye stringer vest mana hai.

**Sawal: Class cancel karni ho to?**
Jawab: Class se kam az kam **3 ghante pehle** cancel karein, warna **no-show** count hoga. Mahine mein **3 no-shows** par **7 din** ke liye class booking band. PT session **12 ghante pehle** cancel karna zaroori hai.

**Sawal: Apna trainer la sakte hain?**
Jawab: **Bahar ke trainers allowed nahi.** Agar koi member paisay le kar doosre member ko train karta pakra gaya to membership **bina refund khatam**.

**Sawal: Kisi aur ko apne fingerprint par andar le jaa sakte hain?**
Jawab: **Bilkul nahi.** Pehli dafa written warning, doosri dafa membership khatam.

**Sawal: Locker ka kya system hai?**
Jawab: Din ke locker free hain, apna taala layein ya desk se PKR 500 mein lein. **Closing tak locker khali karna zaroori hai.** Warna taala kaat diya jata hai aur saman lost & found mein chala jata hai. Monthly locker PKR 1,500 mahana hai, plus PKR 1,000 key deposit.

**Sawal: Shikayat kahan karein?**
Jawab: Pehle branch manager se baat karein, phir **complaints@zorfitness.pk** par email karein. Jawab 3 working days mein milta hai.
