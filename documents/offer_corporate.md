# Corporate Membership Scheme

Zor Fitness Club partners with companies, banks, software houses, NGOs and government offices across Islamabad and Rawalpindi. They get discounted memberships for their staff with one simple monthly or quarterly invoice.

## Discount levels
| Company size (employees joining) | Discount |
|---|---|
| 5 to 19 | **15% off** any plan |
| 20 or more | **20% off** any plan |

The **PKR 10,000 admission fee is waived** for every corporate member, whatever the term.

## Corporate prices per month (PKR)
| Plan | Normal | 15% off | 20% off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard (F-7 / Bahria) | 12,000 | 10,200 | 9,600 |
| Standard (G-11) | 8,000 | 6,800 | 6,400 |
| Plus | 16,000 | 13,600 | 12,800 |
| Elite | 26,000 | 22,100 | 20,800 |
| Ladies Only | 10,000 | 8,500 | 8,000 |

Employees in the same company may choose **different plans**. For example, some staff may take Standard at G-11, others Plus, and female colleagues Ladies Only.

## How it works
1. The company's **HR department emails** hello@zorfitness.pk with the employee list (name, CNIC, chosen plan and home branch for Standard).
2. We confirm the discount level based on the number of employees joining.
3. Employees visit any branch to register their fingerprint, fill in the PAR-Q form and book their free orientation.
4. We send **one invoice to the company** each month or quarter. The company pays by bank transfer.
5. HR can add new employees at any time. The discount level is reviewed each quarter, so if the group grows to 20 or more, the 20% rate applies from the next quarter.

## When an employee leaves
HR must inform us. The employee's corporate membership ends at the end of that billing period. They can then **continue as a normal member at the normal price** from the next billing period, without paying the admission fee.

## Rules
- The corporate discount **cannot be combined** with student, couple, family, senior or Ramadan Fit Pass offers
- Corporate members follow all normal club rules: freeze allowance by term, the guest policy, the dress code and booking rules
- The company, not the employee, is responsible for paying invoices. If a company invoice is unpaid 15 days after its due date, entry for that company's members is suspended.
