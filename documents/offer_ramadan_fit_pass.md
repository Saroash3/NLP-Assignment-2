# Ramadan Fit Pass

Ramadan Mubarak from Zor Fitness Club! Fasting doesn't have to mean stopping your training. Every Ramadan we run the **Ramadan Fit Pass** so new members can start at an easy price.

## The offer
- **One month of the Plus plan for PKR 11,000.** Plus is normally PKR 16,000.
- **Admission fee waived.** It is normally PKR 10,000.
- Includes **all 3 branches** and **all Ramadan night classes**
- Steam and sauna (F-7) and steam (Bahria) are available in the **night session only** during Ramadan

## Conditions
- **New members only.** Existing or previously registered members can't buy it.
- **One pass per CNIC**
- It must be bought and activated **during Ramadan**
- It lasts **1 month** and cannot be frozen
- **Cannot be combined** with student, corporate, couple, family or senior discounts
- After the pass ends you can continue on **any plan at the normal price**, and you still **don't pay the admission fee**, as long as you renew right after the pass ends. If you wait more than 60 days, the normal rejoining rule applies.

## Training while fasting
- The **day session** (before iftar) suits light cardio, mobility and technique work. Avoid hard sessions while dehydrated.
- The **night session** after taraweeh is the best time for strength and intense classes, because you have eaten and can drink water.
- Kamran Baig, our nutritionist, offers Ramadan sehri and iftar meal plans (PKR 4,000 per consultation, or included once a month on Elite).

## Existing members during Ramadan
Existing members keep their normal plan and simply follow the Ramadan hours. They are not eligible for the Fit Pass.
