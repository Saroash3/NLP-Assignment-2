# Free Trial Day, Day Pass and Drop-in Prices

Not ready to commit? Zor Fitness Club offers a free trial day for first-timers, and pay-as-you-go options for visitors and Standard members.

## Free trial day
- **One free trial day** for anyone visiting Zor for the first time, plus a **free InBody body-composition scan**
- **One trial per CNIC.** Bring your original CNIC. Visitors aged 16–17 bring their B-form and a parent.
- Includes a short tour and a warm-up orientation with a trainer on duty
- Women can take their trial on the **F-7 Ladies Floor**, the **Bahria Ladies Wing**, or during **G-11 ladies hours** (Mon–Sat 10:30am–4:30pm)
- The trial covers the gym floor only. It does not include the pool, steam or sauna.
- Book on WhatsApp (0333-5550967) or in the Zor Fitness app, or just walk in during opening hours (last entry is 45 minutes before closing)
- **Special rule:** someone who has used their free trial cannot come back as a member's guest for **7 days** after the trial.

## Day pass: PKR 1,500
- Gym floor access for **one day** at **any** branch
- CNIC required, and you fill in the PAR-Q health form
- **Not included:** group classes, pool, steam, sauna, the kids' play area, and InBody scans
- Day passes are non-refundable and cannot be converted into a membership discount
- Towel rental is PKR 300 if you forget yours, because a towel is mandatory on the gym floor

## Drop-in group class: PKR 1,000 per class
- For Standard members and non-members, subject to space
- Book up to 48 hours ahead. A drop-in booking follows the same 3-hour cancellation rule as member bookings.
- Ladies classes are for women only

## Pool drop-in: PKR 1,500 per swim
At the F-7 pool during normal pool timings. This is for Standard and Plus members, and for Ladies Only members outside their ladies session. A swim cap is mandatory. The pool is closed during Ramadan.

## Tip
If you enjoy your trial, compare the plans on the same day. A 6-month plan avoids the PKR 10,000 admission fee entirely.
