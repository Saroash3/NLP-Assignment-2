# FAQ: Classes and Trainers

**Q: Are classes included in my membership?**
A: **Plus and Elite:** all classes included. **Ladies Only:** all ladies classes included. **Standard:** not included, so pay a **PKR 1,000 drop-in** per class.

**Q: How do I book a class?**
A: In the Zor Fitness app or on WhatsApp 0333-5550967, up to **48 hours** ahead. **Elite members can book 72 hours ahead.**

**Q: What if I'm late?**
A: The studio door closes **10 minutes after the start**. Arriving later counts as a **no-show**.

**Q: What's the penalty for missing classes?**
A: Cancel at least **3 hours** before. **3 no-shows in a calendar month** means a **7-day class-booking block**. You can still use the gym floor during the block.

**Q: Which classes are good for beginners?**
A: **Strength Foundations** (F-7, Mon/Wed 7:00–8:00pm, Hamza Qureshi), **Zor Circuit** (G-11, Mon/Wed/Fri 7:00–7:45am, Usman Tariq), **Spin** (Bahria, Mon/Wed/Fri 6:30–7:15am), and for women, **Yoga Flow**, **Zumba** and **Pilates**.

**Q: Do you have spin classes?**
A: Yes, at **Bahria's 24-bike spin studio** with **Zeeshan Haider**: Spin (Mon/Wed/Fri 6:30–7:15am), Spin Night (Tue/Thu 7:30–8:15pm) and the Sunday Long Ride (Sun 11:00am–12:00pm, intermediate). Members with a heart condition need a doctor's note first.

**Q: Is there boxing?**
A: **Boxing Conditioning** with **Bilal Khattak**, Tue/Thu 8:00–9:00pm at **F-7**, with no sparring. Bilal also runs **HIIT Blast** at Bahria (Tue/Thu/Sat 6:00–6:45pm), which uses the boxing zone.

**Q: Which class runs on Sunday?**
A: Only the **Sunday Long Ride** at Bahria, 11:00am–12:00pm.

**Q: Who is the best trainer for muscle gain?**
A: **Ali Raza**, a bodybuilding coach and provincial-level competitor. He runs the Muscle Building Clinic (F-7, Friday 6:00–7:00pm) and trains PT clients 5:00–10:00pm at G-11 (Mon/Wed/Sat) and F-7 (Tue/Thu/Fri).

**Q: Who should I train with for powerlifting?**
A: Head Coach **Hamza Qureshi** (deadlift PR 260 kg) at F-7. His Powerlifting Club runs **Saturday 9:00–11:00am** for intermediate lifters.

**Q: Is there a female trainer for weight loss at G-11?**
A: **Hira Saleem** coaches during G-11 ladies hours (Mon–Sat, PT 10:30am–4:00pm) and runs Ladies Zor Circuit (Tue/Thu 11:00–11:45am).

**Q: How much does PT cost, and can I switch trainers?**
A: **8 sessions PKR 18,000, 12 for PKR 25,000, 20 for PKR 38,000**, the same for every trainer. Sessions can be moved between Zor trainers. Cancel PT with **12 hours' notice**. If the trainer is over 10 minutes late, that session is free.

**Q: Can I bring my own personal trainer?**
A: No. **Outside trainers are not allowed.** Coaching another member for money leads to termination without refund.

**Q: What's the class size?**
A: Spin 24, Zumba 25, and all others 15. A class with **fewer than 3 bookings** 2 hours before the start may be cancelled.

**Q: Do classes run in Ramadan?**
A: Only 5 night classes: Zor Circuit, HIIT Blast, Spin Night, Ladies Yoga Flow and Ladies Zumba.
