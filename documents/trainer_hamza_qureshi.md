# Trainer Profile: Hamza Qureshi (Head Coach)

**Role:** Head Coach, Strength & Powerlifting
**Branch:** F-7 Islamabad, main floor
**Experience:** 11 years
**Languages:** Urdu, English, Punjabi

## About Hamza
Hamza Qureshi is Zor Fitness Club's Head Coach and has been with the club since the F-7 branch opened in 2019. He is a certified strength and conditioning coach with 11 years of experience, mostly with beginners who want to get strong safely and with competitive powerlifters. His personal best deadlift is **260 kg**, and he still competes in local raw powerlifting meets. As Head Coach he also trains the other Zor trainers and signs off every new trainer's floor programmes.

## Coaching style
Hamza is known for being patient with beginners and strict about technique. His rule is "form before weight": no member adds weight to a lift until he has seen three clean reps. He programmes in 8-week blocks and records every session in the Zor Fitness app, so members can see their progress. For competitive lifters he writes meet-preparation plans and handles attempt selection on competition day.

## Who should train with Hamza?
- Complete beginners who want to learn the squat, bench press and deadlift properly
- Intermediate lifters stuck on a plateau
- Members preparing for a powerlifting competition
- Members returning after a back or knee injury, **after** clearance from physiotherapist Dr. Ayesha Malik. Hamza and Dr. Ayesha often plan return-to-lifting programmes together.
