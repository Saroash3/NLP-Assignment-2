# F-7 Islamabad Branch (Flagship)

**Address:** Plot 14, Jinnah Super Market, F-7 Markaz, Islamabad
**Opened:** 2019. It was Zor Fitness Club's first branch.
**Ladies entrance:** a separate side entrance on College Road, leading directly to the 2nd floor

F-7 is our largest branch, with 3 floors plus a basement pool. It is the only Zor branch with a swimming pool, a sauna and a kids' play area.

## Opening hours
| Area | Mon–Sat | Sunday |
|---|---|---|
| Main floor (men / mixed plans) | 6:00am–11:00pm | 8:00am–8:00pm |
| Ladies Floor (2nd floor) | 7:00am–10:00pm | 10:00am–6:00pm |

Last entry is 45 minutes before closing. Every Friday the whole branch, including the pool, closes 1:00pm–2:15pm for Jumu'ah.

## Floors and facilities
- **Ground and 1st floor, main floor:** cardio machines, free weights with dumbbells up to 50 kg, plate-loaded and pin machines, **6 power racks** and a **deadlift platform**. Liquid chalk is allowed on the platform and the racks only.
- **2nd floor, Ladies Floor:** full cardio and weights equipment, a studio for ladies classes, the ladies steam room and sauna, a ladies prayer room, and the **kids' play area**
- **Basement:** a **20m heated indoor pool** with 4 lanes, heated to 28°C from November to March, plus men's and ladies' changing rooms and **basement parking for 40 cars** (free for 3 hours)
- **Also:** men's steam and sauna, an InBody scanner, a physio room, the **Zor Juice Bar**, and a men's prayer room on the ground floor

## Pool timings
- Men: Mon–Sat 6:00–9:00am and 7:00–10:00pm
- Ladies (female lifeguard): Mon–Sat 11:00am–3:00pm
- Family swim: Sunday 10:00am–2:00pm

## Kids' play area
Free for children aged 3–8 of **any female member (any plan)** while she trains on the Ladies Floor. It is supervised 10:00am–4:00pm, for a maximum of 90 minutes per visit, and the mother must stay in the building.

## Classes at F-7
Strength Foundations (Mon/Wed 7:00–8:00pm), Powerlifting Club (Sat 9:00–11:00am), Boxing Conditioning (Tue/Thu 8:00–9:00pm) and Muscle Building Clinic (Fri 6:00–7:00pm) run on the main floor. Ladies Strength & PCOS Program (Mon/Wed/Fri 11:00am–12:00pm), Yoga Flow (Tue/Thu 10:00–11:00am), Prenatal Yoga (Sat 11:00am–12:00pm) and Zumba (Mon/Wed 5:00–6:00pm) run on the Ladies Floor.

## Price at F-7
Standard with F-7 as home branch costs PKR 12,000 per month. Plus (PKR 16,000), Elite (PKR 26,000) and Ladies Only (PKR 10,000) all include F-7.

## Getting here
F-7 Markaz is in central Islamabad, close to Jinnah Super Market's shops and cafés. There is basement parking, and ride-hailing drop-off is at the main entrance on the market side.
