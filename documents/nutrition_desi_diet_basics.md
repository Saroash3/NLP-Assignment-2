# Desi Diet Basics for Gym-Goers

*General guidance from the Zor Fitness Club team, based on nutritionist Kamran Baig's approach. It is not medical advice. For a personal plan, book a consultation with Kamran (PKR 4,000, or included once a month on Elite).*

You don't need imported "diet food" to get fit. Normal Pakistani home food works very well if you get the **portions** and **balance** right.

## The Zor plate
At lunch and dinner, try to fill your plate like this:
- **Half the plate: sabzi or salad.** Bhindi, lauki, tori, palak, gobhi, or a kachumar salad of cucumber, tomato and onion.
- **A quarter: protein.** Chicken salan (cooked with less oil), daal, chana, lobia, fish, eggs or dahi.
- **A quarter: carbs.** 1–2 rotis or about one cup of cooked chawal.

## Roti and chawal: you don't have to quit them
- Most members do well with **1–2 medium rotis per meal**. Whole-wheat (chakki atta) is better than maida.
- Chawal is fine in measured portions. Pair it with daal or chicken for protein.
- Avoid **paratha every day**. Keep it for weekends, or make it with less ghee.

## Protein at every meal
Protein keeps you full and helps your muscles recover after training. Easy desi sources:
- **Breakfast:** eggs (boiled, omelette), dahi, or chanay
- **Lunch/dinner:** chicken, fish, daal, lobia, rajma, keema made with less oil
- **Snacks:** roasted chanay, a glass of milk, or dahi with fruit
- Our Juice Bar and Café sell boiled eggs (2 for PKR 200) and chicken wraps (PKR 750)

## The hidden problems in a desi diet
1. **Oil and ghee.** Many salans float in oil. Ask for less at home, and skim the oil from the top.
2. **Sugary chai.** 3–4 cups a day with 2 spoons of sugar each adds up. Cut the sugar gradually.
3. **Cold drinks and packaged juice.** Replace them with water, lassi without sugar, or shikanjabeen with less sugar.
4. **Mithai, biscuits and samosas** with chai. Keep them for special occasions.
5. **Late, heavy dinners.** Try to eat dinner at least 2 hours before sleeping.

## Weddings and dawats
Wedding season is hard on any diet. Tips:
- Fill up on salad and chicken tikka or boti first
- Choose one carb: biryani **or** naan, not both
- Take a small portion of dessert, or share it
- Train the next morning. Don't skip the gym to "make up" for it.

## Water
Aim for regular water through the day, more in summer and on training days. Bring a bottle to the gym, because there are no plastic cups at our dispensers.
