# Swimming Pool (F-7 Islamabad Only)

Zor Fitness Club's **20m heated indoor pool** is in the **basement of the F-7 Islamabad branch**. It is the **only pool** at Zor. G-11 and Bahria do not have one.

## Pool details
- **Length:** 20 metres, **4 lanes**
- **Heated to 28°C from November to March**, so you can swim through the Islamabad winter. It is unheated but indoor from April to October.
- Separate men's and ladies' changing rooms with showers
- Lifeguard on duty in every session, with a **female lifeguard** for the ladies session

## Pool timings
| Session | Days | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Men | Mon–Sat | 6:00–9:00am and 7:00–10:00pm |
| **Ladies** | Mon–Sat | **11:00am–3:00pm** |
| **Family swim** | **Sunday** | **10:00am–2:00pm** |

- **Closed Fridays 1:00pm–2:15pm** for Jumu'ah, which falls in the ladies session
- **Closed for the whole of Ramadan**
- Closed on Eid days 1–2 (Eid-ul-Fitr and Eid-ul-Adha)
- Heating may pause during very long load-shedding outages, but the pool stays open

## Who can use the pool?
| Plan | Pool access |
|---|---|
| **Elite** | **All sessions included** (men's, ladies' for women, family swim) |
| **Ladies Only** | **Ladies session + family swim** included |
| Standard, Plus | **PKR 1,500 per swim** |
| Day-pass holders | PKR 1,500 per swim |
| **Guests** | **Not allowed** in the pool, even with a member |

Standard members can buy a pool drop-in only if their home branch is F-7. Plus members can use any branch, so they can swim at F-7 for PKR 1,500.

## Family swim rules (Sunday 10:00am–2:00pm)
- **All ages** are welcome
- **Children under 12 must be in the water with a parent** at all times
- The member's family members must be registered at the desk. Pool fees apply for family members who aren't Elite or Ladies Only members.
- It is the busiest session, so lanes are shared and there is no lap-swimming lane

## Pool rules
- **Swim cap mandatory** for everyone, including children. Caps are sold at the front desk.
- **Shower before entering**
- **Proper swimwear only.** Burkinis and full-length swimsuits are welcome. No cotton t-shirts or gym shorts.
- **No food** on the pool deck, and no glass bottles
- **House rule:** no swimming within **30 minutes after any steam or sauna session**. Shower and cool down first.
- No diving. The pool is 1.2m to 1.8m deep.
- **No photography or filming** in the pool area, at any time

## Swimming lessons
Zor doesn't run group swimming classes. Members can arrange private swim coaching only with Zor staff through PT packages. Outside swim coaches are not allowed, the same as for outside trainers.

## Getting there
Take the lift or stairs to the basement from the main lobby. Women coming from the Ladies Floor use the ladies' stairwell to the ladies changing room, which opens directly onto the pool deck.
