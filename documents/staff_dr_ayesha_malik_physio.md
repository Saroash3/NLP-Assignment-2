## About Dr. Ayesha
Dr. Ayesha Malik is Zor Fitness Club's in-house physiotherapist. She holds a **DPT** and specialises in **sports injuries, back and neck pain, knee problems, and returning to exercise safely** after injury or surgery. She is a female physiotherapist, which many of our female members prefer, but she treats **both men and women**. She works closely with the trainers. For example, she plans return-to-lifting programmes with Head Coach Hamza Qureshi, and postnatal core programmes with Mahnoor Rizvi and Sana Iftikhar.

## Common reasons members book
- Lower back pain from desk jobs or incorrect deadlift technique
- Knee pain when squatting or running
- Shoulder impingement from pressing
- Recovery after ACL surgery or a sprain, once cleared by your surgeon
- **Postpartum** pelvic floor and diastasis (tummy gap) assessment
- Posture assessment for students and office workers

## How to book
Book in the **Zor Fitness app** or on **WhatsApp 0333-5550967**, choosing the branch and day. Bring any X-rays, MRI reports or doctor's notes. Wear comfortable clothing. Female clients can ask for a chaperone.

## Medical freeze
If Dr. Ayesha or your doctor advises you to stop training, members on plans of **3 months or longer** can take a **medical freeze** of up to **60 days**, free, with a doctor's certificate. It is in addition to the normal freeze allowance. Monthly plans can't be frozen.

## Important
Physiotherapy at Zor is for musculoskeletal problems. Dr. Ayesha does not prescribe medicines. For medical emergencies, chest pain or serious injuries, go to a hospital. The staff are first-aid trained, and each branch has an AED.
