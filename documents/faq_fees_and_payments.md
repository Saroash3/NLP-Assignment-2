# FAQ: Fees, Prices and Payments

**Q: What are your monthly prices?**
A: **Standard** PKR 12,000 at F-7 and Bahria, or PKR 8,000 at G-11 (home branch only). **Plus** PKR 16,000 (all branches, all classes). **Elite** PKR 26,000 (includes PT, nutrition, pool and guest passes). **Ladies Only** PKR 10,000.

**Q: Why is G-11 cheaper?**
A: G-11 is our compact branch: one shared floor, 2 power racks, dumbbells up to 40 kg, no pool, no steam or sauna, and closed on Sundays.

**Q: Is it cheaper to pay for several months?**
A: Yes. **3 months: 10% off. 6 months: 15% off and no admission fee. 12 months: 25% off and no admission fee.** For example, Plus for 12 months is PKR 144,000 instead of paying monthly plus a PKR 10,000 admission fee.

**Q: Can I pay in instalments?**
A: Only **12-month plans**, in **2 equal instalments**. The second is due on the **first day of month 7**. More than 7 days late adds a **PKR 1,500 late fee**. After **15 days** unpaid, entry is suspended and those days are not added back.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: Cash, debit or credit card (**no surcharge**, even over PKR 50,000), bank transfer, **JazzCash** and **Easypaisa**. **We don't accept cheques.** The Juice Bar and Café take card or JazzCash only.

**Q: Can I combine the student and family discounts?**
A: No. **Only one discount applies at a time.** Discounts never stack.

**Q: How much is a day pass?**
A: **PKR 1,500** for the gym floor at any branch (CNIC required). A drop-in class is **PKR 1,000**, and a pool swim at F-7 is **PKR 1,500**.

**Q: How much is a guest pass?**
A: **PKR 1,000** per visit. Elite members get 2 free guest passes per month.

**Q: How much is personal training?**
A: **8 sessions PKR 18,000, 12 sessions PKR 25,000, 20 sessions PKR 38,000.** The price is the same for every trainer, and packages are valid for 60 days.

**Q: What other charges might I see?**
A: Monthly locker PKR 1,500 (plus a PKR 1,000 refundable key deposit), padlock PKR 500, towel PKR 300 per day or PKR 2,000 per month, replacement member card PKR 1,000, InBody scan PKR 800, physio assessment PKR 3,500, and nutrition consultation PKR 4,000.

**Q: My company has 25 employees. What discount do we get?**
A: **20% off any plan** (20+ employees), with the admission fee waived and one invoice to the company. 5–19 employees get 15%.

**Q: Do I get a receipt?**
A: Yes. Every payment shows in the Zor Fitness app. Corporate members' companies get one combined invoice.

**Q: Will the price change if I renew?**
A: You pay the price in effect at renewal. If you've been inactive for more than 60 days, the admission fee applies again, unless you take a 6 or 12-month plan.
