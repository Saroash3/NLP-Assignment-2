## Rules
- **Maximum 15 minutes** per session
- **Age 16+ only**
- **Not allowed during pregnancy**
- **Not while fasting.** During Ramadan, steam and sauna open **only in the night session**, after iftar.
- They **close 30 minutes before** the floor closes. For example, the F-7 main-floor steam closes at 10:30pm on weekdays, and the Bahria steam at 11:30pm.
- Wear **swimwear or a towel wrap**. **No gym shoes** inside. Slippers are fine up to the door.
- Shower before entering
- Drink water before and after, and leave immediately if you feel dizzy or faint
- No phones inside, because of the heat and privacy. **No photography or filming.**
- **Pool house rule (F-7):** no swimming within **30 minutes** after any steam or sauna session

## Health advice
Steam and sauna are relaxing after training, but they aren't suitable for everyone. Check with your doctor first if you have:
- High or low blood pressure
- A heart condition
- Diabetes
- Skin conditions, or recent surgery

Seniors (60+) should ask their doctor before using the steam or sauna. Staff are first-aid trained, and every branch has an AED.

## Steam vs sauna: what's the difference?
- **Steam room:** moist heat at a lower temperature, around 45°C, with close to 100% humidity. Many members like it for breathing and relaxation, especially in the dry Islamabad winter.
- **Sauna:** dry heat, hotter at around 80°C, with low humidity. It feels more intense, so start with 5–8 minutes.

## Hygiene
- Sit on a towel, never directly on the benches
- No shaving, no hair colour or mehndi, and no oils or scrubs inside
- Rooms are cleaned hourly along with the changing rooms

## During load-shedding
Steam and sauna heaters run on the generator during normal outages. During outages longer than 2 hours they may be switched off to save fuel. Check at the front desk.
