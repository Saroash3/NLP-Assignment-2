# Trainer Profile: Sana Iftikhar

**Role:** Women's Strength Coach, PCOS and hormonal health
**Branch:** F-7 Islamabad, Ladies Floor (2nd floor)
**Experience:** 7 years
**Languages:** Urdu, English

## About Sana
Sana Iftikhar is a **former national-level netball player** who moved into coaching after her playing career. She has 7 years of experience training women and specialises in strength training for **PCOS, insulin resistance and hormonal weight gain**. She designed Zor's **Ladies Strength & PCOS Program** after seeing how many members struggled with generic "cardio only" advice. Sana believes most women benefit from lifting weights, and her sessions focus on building confidence with dumbbells, cables and the squat rack on the Ladies Floor.

## Class Sana teaches
| Class | Days | Time | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ladies Strength & PCOS Program | Mon, Wed, Fri | 11:00am–12:00pm | F-7 Ladies Floor |

The class is all levels, with a capacity of 15. It combines resistance training, low-impact conditioning and short talks on habits like sleep, steps and consistency. On Fridays it finishes well before the 1:00pm Jumu'ah closure.

## Personal training availability
- **Mon–Sat: 8:00–11:00am and 2:00–5:00pm**
- Sunday: not available

PT prices are the same for all trainers: **8 sessions PKR 18,000 · 12 sessions PKR 25,000 · 20 sessions PKR 38,000**. The sessions are valid for 60 days. Elite members can use their 4 monthly sessions with Sana.

## Working with the nutritionist
Sana works closely with **Kamran Baig**, Zor's registered dietitian. Members in the PCOS program often book a nutrition consultation (PKR 4,000, or included once a month on Elite) so their training and desi-diet plan support each other. Sana and Kamran share progress notes (with the member's permission) through the app.

## Important note
Sana's program is **fitness support, not medical treatment**. She does not diagnose PCOS or change medication. Members should follow their gynaecologist's or endocrinologist's advice, and declare their condition on the PAR-Q form.

## Who should train with Sana?
- Women with PCOS or hormonal weight gain who want a structured strength plan
- Women who feel nervous about the weights area and want a female coach
- Mothers returning to training after the postpartum period, with doctor's clearance
- Athletes (netball, basketball, badminton) wanting sport-specific strength
