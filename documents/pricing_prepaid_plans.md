# Prepaid Plan Prices and Discounts (3, 6 and 12 Months)

Paying in advance is the cheapest way to train at Zor Fitness Club. The longer you commit, the bigger the discount, and from 6 months the admission fee disappears.

## Prepaid discount rules
- **3 months:** 10% off the monthly price. The PKR 10,000 admission fee **still applies**.
- **6 months:** 15% off, and the admission fee is **waived**.
- **12 months:** 25% off, and the admission fee is **waived**. It can be paid in 2 instalments.

## Worked example
A new member choosing Plus:
- Month by month for a year: 12 × PKR 16,000, plus the PKR 10,000 admission fee
- 12-month prepaid: **PKR 144,000** with no admission fee. That saves a quarter of the monthly price **and** the joining fee.

## Extra benefits that only prepaid plans get
- **Freeze allowance:** 3 months = 7 days, 6 months = 15 days, 12 months = 30 days. **Monthly plans cannot be frozen at all.** Each freeze must be at least 7 days, with a maximum of 2 separate freezes per plan.
- **Medical freeze** of up to 60 days with a doctor's certificate, on plans of 3 months or longer.
- **Student exam freeze** of 30 days once a year, on 3-month-plus plans.
- **Relocation refund:** on 6-month and 12-month plans only, if you move more than 50 km from every branch. You get a pro-rata refund of unused full months minus a 10% processing fee.
- **Family transfer:** on 12-month plans only, a one-time transfer to an immediate family member for PKR 3,000, with at least 3 months left.
- **Azadi Offer:** 12-month plans bought 1–14 August get 1 extra month free (13 months in total) and a free green Zor tracksuit.

## Instalments for 12-month plans
A 12-month plan can be split into **2 equal instalments**. For example, Elite at PKR 234,000 becomes 2 × PKR 117,000. The second instalment is due on the **first day of month 7**. If it is more than 7 days late, a **PKR 1,500 late fee** is added. After 15 days unpaid, entry is suspended, and those days are not added back to your plan. See the instalments and late fee document.

## Important
Prepaid plans are **non-refundable** except under the 7-day cooling-off, relocation and permanent-medical rules. Discounts do not stack: if you use a student, family, couple, corporate or senior discount, it is applied to the monthly price instead of a second discount being added on top.
