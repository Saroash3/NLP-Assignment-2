# Equipment, Parking, Prayer Rooms and Other Amenities

## Equipment by branch
| | F-7 Islamabad | G-11 Islamabad | Bahria Town Phase 7 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Power racks | **6** (main floor) | **2** | **4** |
| Deadlift platform | Yes (bumper plates) | No | No |
| Dumbbells up to | **50 kg** | **40 kg** | 50 kg |
| Cardio machines | Treadmills, bikes, ellipticals, rowers, stairmaster | Treadmills, bikes, ellipticals | Treadmills, bikes, ellipticals, rowers |
| Special areas | 20m pool, Ladies Floor | 1 studio | **Spin studio (24 bikes)**, boxing zone (4 heavy bags + ring), Ladies Wing |
| Steam / sauna | Both (men and ladies) | None | Steam only (men and ladies) |

The Ladies Floor (F-7) and Ladies Wing (Bahria) have their own full sets of cardio machines, dumbbells, cable machines and a squat rack.

## Parking
- **F-7:** **basement parking for 40 cars, free for 3 hours**. After that, the building's parking charges apply. It fills up in the evenings, so there is also paid street parking in F-7 Markaz.
- **G-11:** **street parking only**. There is no building parking. Arrive early for 7:00am classes.
- **Bahria:** **free open parking** in front of the building

## Prayer facilities
- **F-7:** separate prayer rooms, the men's on the **ground floor** and the ladies' on the **2nd floor** (Ladies Floor)
- **Bahria:** separate men's and ladies' prayer rooms
- **G-11:** a prayer area
- Wudu facilities are next to the changing rooms
- The club closes **1:00pm–2:15pm every Friday** for Jumu'ah

## Wi-Fi
Free Wi-Fi at all branches. **The password is at the front desk.** Video calls are not allowed on the gym floor, and phone calls must be taken in the lobby.

## Water
Water dispensers are on every floor. **Bring your own bottle**, because there are **no plastic cups**. Protein powders must not be mixed at the dispensers.

## Power backup
All branches have **generators and UPS**, so we stay open during load-shedding. During outages longer than 2 hours the AC runs at reduced power, and the F-7 lift may stop.

## Changing rooms
- Separate men's and ladies' changing rooms with hot showers at every branch
- Cleaned hourly
- **Free day lockers**: bring a padlock or buy one for PKR 500. Emptied at closing.
- **Monthly lockers**: PKR 1,500 per month plus a PKR 1,000 refundable key deposit
- **Towel rental**: PKR 300 per day or PKR 2,000 per month. Elite members get 1 free towel per visit.

## Accessibility
F-7 has a lift to all floors, including the basement pool, except during long power outages. Bahria's main floor and Ladies Wing are on the ground floor. **G-11 is on the 2nd floor of Al-Hafeez Plaza**, reached by the plaza lift. Members with mobility needs should speak to the branch manager, who can arrange assisted entry.
