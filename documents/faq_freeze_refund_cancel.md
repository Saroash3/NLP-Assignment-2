# FAQ: Freezing, Refunds and Cancelling

**Q: I'm going abroad for 2 weeks. Can I freeze my monthly plan?**
A: No. **Monthly plans can't be frozen.** Only 3-month (7 days), 6-month (15 days) and 12-month (30 days) plans have a freeze allowance. On a monthly plan, just don't renew. If you come back within 60 days, there is no admission fee.

**Q: How do I request a freeze?**
A: At the front desk, in the app (Profile → Freeze), or on WhatsApp 0333-5550967, **at least 48 hours before** the freeze starts. The **minimum is 7 days**, with a **maximum of 2 freezes per plan**, and **no backdating**.

**Q: I forgot to freeze before my trip. Can you freeze it now for the days I missed?**
A: Sorry, no. Freezes **can't be backdated**. Only future days can be frozen.

**Q: I broke my wrist. Do I lose my membership time?**
A: If you're on a plan of **3 months or longer**, you can take a **medical freeze of up to 60 days**, free, with a doctor's certificate. It's on top of your normal allowance. In an emergency, send the certificate on WhatsApp within 7 days.

**Q: Can I use the gym as a guest while frozen?**
A: No. Frozen members **can't enter at all**, not even as someone's guest.

**Q: I joined 3 days ago but changed my mind. Can I get a refund?**
A: Only if you **haven't visited even once**. We check the fingerprint log. If so, the plan fee is refunded, but **the PKR 10,000 admission fee is never refunded**.

**Q: I'm moving to Karachi for work. What about my 12-month plan?**
A: Moving **more than 50 km from all branches** qualifies for a relocation refund on **6-month or 12-month plans**: the unused **full months** minus a **10% processing fee**. Bring proof of your new address (a utility bill or employer letter). The refund arrives within **14 working days**. Cash payments are refunded by bank transfer.

**Q: I'm moving from F-7 to Rawalpindi. Do I get a refund?**
A: No. That's within 50 km. If you're on Standard, **change your home branch** to Bahria (free once per quarter), or upgrade to Plus for all branches.

**Q: Can I get my unused PT sessions refunded?**
A: No. **PT packages are non-refundable**, and they expire 60 days after purchase. You can move sessions between Zor trainers, but not to another person.

**Q: Can my sister take over my membership?**
A: Only a **12-month plan**, **once**, to immediate family (spouse, parent, child, sibling), for **PKR 3,000**, with at least **3 months left**.

**Q: How do I cancel my membership?**
A: Monthly plans have **no contract and no auto-renewal**, so simply don't renew. Prepaid plans can't be cancelled for a refund except under the three refund exceptions (cooling-off, relocation, or permanent medical inability).
