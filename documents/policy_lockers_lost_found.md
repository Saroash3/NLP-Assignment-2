# Locker Rules and Lost & Found

## Day lockers (free)
- Every branch has **free day lockers** in the men's and ladies' changing rooms
- **Bring your own padlock**, or buy one at the front desk for **PKR 500**
- Day lockers are for use **during your visit only**
- **All day lockers must be emptied by closing time.** After closing, staff **cut the locks** on any locker that is still locked. The contents go to **lost & found**, and the lock is not replaced.
- Don't leave bags on the gym floor. Bags, jackets and backpacks must go in a locker, both for safety and to keep walkways clear.

## Monthly lockers (rental)
- **PKR 1,500 per month**, rented at the front desk
- Plus a **PKR 1,000 refundable key deposit**, returned when you give back the key in good condition
- **Lost key: PKR 1,000.** The deposit is kept to replace the lock and key.
- A monthly locker is at **one branch only**. Standard members rent at their home branch.
- If the rent is not paid within **5 days** of its due date, the locker is emptied and the contents go to lost & found
- A monthly locker is personal. Sharing it with another member isn't allowed.
- Monthly lockers are not frozen when your membership is frozen. Keep paying, or give it back.

## What not to leave in any locker
- **Cash, jewellery, watches, phones, laptops or car keys overnight**
- Wet towels or clothes for long periods, because of smell and hygiene. Staff may clear monthly lockers that smell.
- **Zor Fitness Club is not responsible for valuables** lost from any locker, day or monthly. There is a safe at the front desk for car keys and phones during your visit only.

## Lost & found
- Items found in the club, including contents cleared from day lockers at closing, are kept at the branch front desk for **30 days**
- After 30 days, unclaimed items are **donated** to charity. Items with no resale value are discarded.
- Describe your item to the front desk, or message WhatsApp 0333-5550967 with the branch name
- ID documents (CNIC, cards) are kept separately and returned to the owner, or handed to the police after 30 days

## Water bottles
Bring your own bottle. There are **no plastic cups** at the water dispensers, and **mixing protein powder at the water dispensers is not allowed**, because it clogs the taps. Use the sink in the changing room or buy a shake at the Juice Bar or Café.

## Member cards
Lost member card? A replacement costs **PKR 1,000**. Entry mainly uses your fingerprint, so you can still train while you wait for the new card.
