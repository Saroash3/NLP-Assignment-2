# Health and Safety Policy

## PAR-Q health declaration
Every member, day-pass holder and guest completes a **PAR-Q** (Physical Activity Readiness Questionnaire) before training. You must declare:
- Heart problems, chest pain, high blood pressure or dizziness
- Diabetes, asthma or epilepsy
- Recent surgery, fractures or joint replacements
- **Pregnancy**
- Any medicine that affects heart rate or balance

Giving false information on the PAR-Q can lead to termination.

## When a doctor's note is required
| Situation | Requirement |
|---|---|
| A heart condition and wanting to join **HIIT Blast** or **any Spin class** | A doctor's note first |
| **Prenatal Yoga** | A doctor's clearance, renewed each trimester |
| Seniors (60+) with heart conditions, or where the physio screening recommends it | A doctor's note before starting |
| **Medical freeze** (up to 60 days) | A doctor's certificate |
| **Permanent medical inability refund** | A doctor's certificate stating it is permanent |

## Steam room and sauna rules
- **Maximum 15 minutes** per session
- **16+ only**
- **Not allowed during pregnancy**
- Not allowed while fasting (Ramadan night session only)
- Steam and sauna close **30 minutes before** the floor closes
- Drink water before and after. Leave immediately if you feel faint.
- **Pool house rule (F-7):** no swimming within **30 minutes** after any steam or sauna session. Shower and cool down first.

## Pool safety (F-7)
- A lifeguard is on duty in every session. The ladies session has a **female lifeguard**.
- A swim cap is mandatory, and you must shower before entering
- Children under 12 must be in the water with a parent during family swim

## Emergency equipment and staff
- **All staff are first-aid trained**
- There is an **AED (defibrillator) at every branch**: F-7, G-11 and Bahria
- First-aid kits are at every front desk and studio
- In an emergency, staff call Rescue 1122 and inform your emergency contact from the app

## Training safely
- Use safety pins or a spotter for heavy bench press and squats
- Stop immediately if you feel chest pain, severe dizziness or shortness of breath, and tell staff
- In **summer**, the AC may run at reduced power during load-shedding outages longer than 2 hours. Hydrate more and lower the intensity.
- In **Ramadan**, train lightly in the day session and save intense training for the night session after iftar

## Hygiene
- A towel is mandatory on the gym floor
- Wipe down equipment after use
- Don't train with contagious illnesses such as flu, conjunctivitis or skin infections. Use the medical freeze on plans of 3 months or longer if needed.
- Changing rooms are cleaned hourly. G-11's floor is fully cleaned 10:00–10:30am and 4:30–5:00pm.

## Injuries
Report any injury on the premises to the front desk, where it is recorded in the incident log. Physiotherapist **Dr. Ayesha Malik** can assess injuries by appointment (PKR 3,500, 20% off for members).
