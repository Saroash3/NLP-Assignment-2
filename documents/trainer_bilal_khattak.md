# Trainer Profile: Bilal Khattak

**Role:** HIIT and Boxing Conditioning Coach
**Branches:** Bahria Town Phase 7 (Mon–Sat), plus F-7 Islamabad on Tue/Thu evenings for one class
**Background:** Former amateur boxer from Peshawar
**Languages:** Pashto, Urdu, English

## About Bilal
Bilal Khattak grew up in Peshawar and boxed at the amateur level for several years before moving into fitness coaching. He brings a boxer's work ethic and conditioning methods to Zor Fitness Club. His classes are tough but well structured, and he always offers a lower-impact version of every exercise, so intermediate members can keep up without injury. He built the boxing zone at Bahria: 4 heavy bags and a ring.

## Personal training availability
- **Bahria: Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 4:00–9:00pm**
- He does not take PT sessions at F-7.

PT packages cost the same as for every trainer: **8 sessions PKR 18,000 · 12 sessions PKR 25,000 · 20 sessions PKR 38,000**, valid for 60 days. Elite members can use their 4 monthly sessions with Bilal. His PT sessions usually include pad work, footwork drills and conditioning circuits.

## Boxing at Zor: what to expect
- **Technique first:** stance, jab, cross, hooks, and slipping
- **Pad work in pairs** in Boxing Conditioning. **There is no sparring** in classes or PT.
- Gloves are provided, but **bring your own hand wraps**. Many regulars buy their own gloves for hygiene.
- Conditioning finishers: skipping, burpees and heavy-bag rounds

## Health rules
Members with a **heart condition** must give a **doctor's note** before joining HIIT Blast. Bilal checks the PAR-Q form of every new attendee. Stop and tell him if you feel dizzy or have chest pain. Every branch has an AED, and all staff are first-aid trained.

## Who should train with Bilal?
- Members who want intense, fast workouts for fat loss and fitness
- Anyone curious about boxing technique without contact sparring
- Athletes (cricket, football) wanting better conditioning and agility
- Office workers wanting to de-stress. Hitting the heavy bag helps.

## Good to know
Bilal's classes are main-floor classes for Plus and Elite members (free) and Standard members (PKR 1,000 drop-in). Women on mixed plans are welcome. Bring a towel, which is mandatory, and water.
