# Beginner's Guide to Your First Month at the Gym

*General guidance from the Zor Fitness Club coaching team. If you have a medical condition, declare it on your PAR-Q form and follow your doctor's advice.*

Starting at a gym can feel intimidating. This guide explains what to do in your first 4 weeks at Zor.

## Day one
1. Take your **free 30-minute floor orientation**. A trainer shows you the machines, the power racks, and the floor rules.
2. Do your **InBody scan** so you have a starting point.
3. Download the **Zor Fitness app** to book classes and track progress.
4. Bring a **towel** (mandatory), a water bottle, and **closed sports shoes**.

## How often should I train?
- **Weeks 1–2:** 3 sessions a week, for example Monday, Wednesday and Friday, with rest days in between
- **Weeks 3–4:** 3–4 sessions a week
- Each session: 45–60 minutes, including a warm-up and a cool-down
- Rest days matter. Muscles grow and recover when you rest.

## A simple full-body beginner session
- **Warm-up (10 min):** brisk treadmill walk or bike, then arm circles, leg swings and bodyweight squats
- **Main workout** (2–3 sets of 10–12 reps each, with light weights and good form):
  - Leg press or goblet squat
  - Chest press machine or push-ups (on your knees is fine)
  - Seated cable row or lat pulldown
  - Dumbbell Romanian deadlift (learn the hip hinge first)
  - Plank, 20–30 seconds
- **Cardio (10 min):** bike, elliptical or incline walk
- **Cool-down (5 min):** stretching

## Common beginner mistakes
- **Lifting too heavy too soon.** Hamza's rule is "form before weight": 3 clean reps before adding weight.
- **Only doing cardio.** Strength training helps fat loss and posture too.
- **Copying advanced lifters on social media.** Follow a simple plan.
- **Skipping the warm-up**
- **Training through sharp pain.** Stop and speak to a trainer, or book physio Dr. Ayesha Malik.

## Soreness
Feeling sore 1–2 days after a new workout is normal. It is called DOMS. Light walking and stretching help. Sharp or joint pain is **not** normal, so get it checked.

## Gym etiquette for beginners
Re-rack weights, wipe equipment, no phone calls on the floor, and at peak times (weekdays 6:00–9:00pm) there is a 20-minute limit on cardio machines and a 30-minute limit on racks when others are waiting. If you're nervous, weekday mid-mornings are the quietest.
