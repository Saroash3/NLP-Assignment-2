# FAQ (Roman Urdu): Fees aur Timings

**Sawal: Gym ki monthly fee kitni hai?**
Jawab: Har plan ki mahana fee alag hai:
- **Standard:** F-7 aur Bahria mein **PKR 12,000**, G-11 mein **PKR 8,000** (sirf Standard ki fee branch ke hisaab se badalti hai)
- **Plus:** **PKR 16,000** -- teeno branches mein aik hi fee, saari classes shamil
- **Elite:** **PKR 26,000** -- teeno branches mein aik hi fee
- **Ladies Only:** **PKR 10,000** -- teeno branches mein aik hi fee

**Sawal: Admission fee kitni hai?**
Jawab: Aik dafa ki **admission fee PKR 10,000** hai. Agar aap **6 ya 12 mahine** ka plan lein to admission fee **maaf** hai.

**Sawal: Kya 3 mahine ka package sasta parta hai?**
Jawab: Ji haan. 3 mahine par **10% discount**, 6 mahine par **15%**, aur 12 mahine par **25%** discount hai. Maslan Plus 12 mahine ke liye PKR 144,000.

**Sawal: Kya qiston mein payment ho sakti hai?**
Jawab: Sirf **12 mahine** wale plan mein **2 barabar qiston** mein. Doosri qist **7th mahine ke pehle din** due hoti hai. 7 din late hone par **PKR 1,500 late fee** lagti hai, aur 15 din ke baad entry band ho jati hai.

**Sawal: Gym kab khulta hai?**
Jawab: **F-7:** Mon–Sat 6:00am–11:00pm, Itwar 8:00am–8:00pm. **Bahria:** Mon–Sat 6:00am–12:00am (raat 12 baje tak), Itwar 10:00am–8:00pm. **G-11:** mardon ke liye 6:00–10:00am aur 5:00–11:00pm, **Itwar ko band**.

**Sawal: Ladies ki timing kya hai?**
Jawab: **F-7 Ladies Floor** Mon–Sat 7:00am–10:00pm aur Itwar 10:00am–6:00pm. **Bahria Ladies Wing** Mon–Sat 8:00am–9:00pm (Itwar band). **G-11** mein ladies timing 10:30am–4:30pm hai.

**Sawal: Jummay ke din gym band hota hai?**
Jawab: Jummay ko **1:00pm se 2:15pm** tak teeno branches Jumu'ah ki namaz ke liye band hoti hain. Is waqt koi class nahi hoti.

**Sawal: Student discount milta hai?**
Jawab: Ji, **16 se 25 saal** ke students ko valid student card par Standard ya Plus par **25% discount** milta hai. Lekin entry sirf **off-peak: Mon–Fri 6:00am–4:00pm**. G-11 Standard sirf PKR 6,000 mahana parta hai.

**Sawal: Ek din ke liye aa sakte hain?**
Jawab: **Day pass PKR 1,500** ka hai, CNIC zaroori hai. Pehli dafa aane walon ke liye **aik free trial day** bhi hai.

**Sawal: Mehmaan (guest) la sakte hain?**
Jawab: Ji, **PKR 1,000** fi visit. Guest ki umar 16+ honi chahiye aur CNIC zaroori hai. Mahine mein zyada se zyada **4 guest visits**, aur aik hi guest mahine mein sirf **2 dafa**. **G-11 mein Mon–Fri 6:00–9:00pm guest allowed nahi.**

**Sawal: Eid par gym khula hota hai?**
Jawab: **Eid-ul-Fitr aur Eid-ul-Adha ke pehle 2 din** band hota hai. Yeh din aap ki membership mein **khud-ba-khud add** ho jate hain.

**Sawal: Payment kaise karein?**
Jawab: Cash, card (koi extra charge nahi), bank transfer, **JazzCash** ya **Easypaisa**. **Cheque qubool nahi.**
