# Who Can Join: Men and Women at Zor Fitness Club

Zor Fitness Club is a **mixed gym for men and women**. It is **not** a ladies-only gym. Men are welcome at all three branches, and women can choose either a mixed plan or the women-only plan.

## Plans for men
Men can join **three plans: Standard, Plus or Elite**. Men **cannot** join the Ladies Only plan.
- **Standard:** PKR 12,000 per month at F-7 and Bahria, PKR 8,000 at G-11. Gym floor at your home branch only.
- **Plus:** PKR 16,000 per month. All 3 branches and all group classes (ladies classes are for women only).
- **Elite:** PKR 26,000 per month. Everything in Plus, plus 4 personal training sessions and a nutrition consultation every month, and pool access at F-7.

The **Ladies Only** plan (PKR 10,000) is the only plan that is for women only.

## Gym timings for men: when can males train?
Men's timings (regular hours, not Ramadan) at each branch:
- **F-7 Islamabad:** the main floor, Mon–Sat 6:00am–11:00pm and Sun 8:00am–8:00pm. Men's pool sessions are Mon–Sat 6:00–9:00am and 7:00–10:00pm.
- **Bahria Town Phase 7, Rawalpindi:** the main floor, Mon–Sat 6:00am–12:00am (midnight) and Sun 10:00am–8:00pm.
- **G-11 Islamabad:** one shared floor. Men's hours are Mon–Sat 6:00–10:00am and 5:00–11:00pm. From 10:30am to 4:30pm the floor is women-only. G-11 is closed on Sundays.

## Plans for women
Which plans are for women? **All four.** Women can join **any of the four plans**:
- **Ladies Only** gives access to the women's areas at all three branches and all ladies classes.
- **Standard, Plus or Elite** gives a female member **both** the main floor and the women's areas at the branches her plan covers.

## Women-only spaces
F-7 has a separate Ladies Floor and Bahria has a separate Ladies Wing, each with its own entrance and female trainers. Ladies classes are for women only, and male trainers do not coach in the women's areas. These spaces exist so that women who prefer to train separately can do so; the rest of the gym is open to everyone.

## Quick answers
- **Can men join Zor?** Yes. Choose Standard, Plus or Elite.
- **Is Zor only for ladies?** No. It is a mixed gym with separate women-only areas.
- **Can a couple join together?** Yes. Married couples who both join Plus or Elite get 10% off each.
