# Refer-a-Friend Programme

Your friends are the best gym partners. Zor Fitness Club rewards members who bring new people into the club.

## The reward
When a friend you referred **joins on a 3-month or longer plan**, you get **2 weeks added** to your membership.

- **Maximum 3 referrals per year** (per calendar year), so up to **6 extra weeks**
- The extra time is added **at your next renewal**, at the end of your new plan
- Rewards are **time only**. They are **never paid as cash** and can't be exchanged for PT sessions, café credit or merchandise.

## How it works
1. Your friend visits any branch, or books a free trial day on WhatsApp (0333-5550967).
2. When joining, your friend gives your **name or member number** to the front desk. It must be given at the time of joining and can't be added later.
3. Your friend chooses a **3-month, 6-month or 12-month plan** on any plan type: Standard, Plus, Elite or Ladies Only.
4. Once your friend's **7-day cooling-off period** has passed without a refund, your 2 weeks are confirmed in the Zor Fitness app.

## Who counts as a referral?
- **New members only.** Someone who has been a member before and was inactive for less than 60 days doesn't count.
- A friend who joins on a **1-month plan** or buys a day pass doesn't count. If they later upgrade to a 3-month plan at their first renewal, it still counts, as long as they gave your name at joining.
- Family members joining on the family discount **do count**, and so do colleagues joining through the corporate scheme.
- Your friend's own discount (student, corporate, etc.) doesn't affect your reward.

## Rules
- You must be an **active member** when the reward is added. Inactive or suspended members (for example, with an unpaid instalment) lose pending rewards.
- The extra weeks can't be transferred to another person, but they stay on a 12-month plan that is transferred to family.
- A referral reward can't be combined with the Azadi Offer's extra month for the same purchase. Both extensions are added separately if they come from different purchases.
- If your friend gets a relocation or medical refund later, your reward is not removed.

## What your friend gets
Your friend gets the normal joining terms: a free trial day and InBody scan, a free 30-minute orientation, and any discount they personally qualify for.

## Checking your rewards
Open the Zor Fitness app → Profile → Referrals, or ask the front desk. For questions, email hello@zorfitness.pk.
