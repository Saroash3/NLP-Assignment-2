# FAQ: Ramadan, Eid and Holidays

**Q: What are the gym timings in Ramadan?**
A: **Men / main floors:** **10:00am–3:00pm** and **9:30pm–2:00am**. **Ladies** (F-7 Ladies Floor, Bahria Ladies Wing, G-11 ladies): **11:00am–3:00pm** and **9:00pm–12:00am**. The night session starts after taraweeh.

**Q: Ramadan mein gym kab khulta hai? (When does the gym open in Ramadan?)**
A: Mardon ke liye din mein 10:00am–3:00pm aur raat ko taraweeh ke baad 9:30pm–2:00am. Khawateen ke liye 11:00am–3:00pm aur 9:00pm–12:00am.

**Q: Is the pool open in Ramadan?**
A: No. **The pool is closed for the whole of Ramadan.**

**Q: Can I use the steam room while fasting?**
A: No. **Steam and sauna open only in the night session** during Ramadan, because of the dehydration risk.

**Q: Which classes run in Ramadan?**
A: Only 5, all at night: **Zor Circuit** (G-11, Mon/Wed/Fri 10:00–10:45pm), **HIIT Blast** (Bahria, Tue/Thu 10:30–11:15pm), **Spin Night** (Bahria, Mon/Wed/Fri 11:00–11:45pm), **Ladies Yoga Flow** (F-7, Tue/Thu 9:30–10:15pm), and **Ladies Zumba** (Bahria Ladies Wing, Tue/Thu 9:30–10:15pm).

**Q: Is there a Ramadan offer?**
A: The **Ramadan Fit Pass**: **1 month of Plus for PKR 11,000**, with **no admission fee**. It's for new members only, one per CNIC, and can't be frozen.

**Q: Does my membership pause automatically in Ramadan?**
A: No. **Ramadan is not an automatic freeze.** If you want a break, use your normal allowance: 7, 15 or 30 days depending on your plan length, requested 48 hours ahead.

**Q: Is the gym open on Eid?**
A: **Closed on Eid-ul-Fitr days 1–2 and Eid-ul-Adha days 1–2.** Those days are **added to your membership automatically**.

**Q: When do normal classes start again after Ramadan?**
A: On the **first working day after the Eid-ul-Fitr closure**.

**Q: What about 14 August and 23 March?**
A: Open **10:00am–6:00pm** on all floors. These shortened days are **not** added back to memberships. Classes normally don't run on public holidays. On 14 August you can still buy the **Azadi Offer** (12-month plans bought 1–14 August get 1 extra month free and a green Zor tracksuit). It is the last day of the offer.

**Q: Is the gym open during Friday prayers?**
A: No. All branches close **1:00pm–2:15pm every Friday** for Jumu'ah.

**Q: What if there's a lockdown or an emergency closure?**
A: Full closures ordered by the government are **added to your membership automatically**, just like Eid.

**Q: Is the gym open during load-shedding?**
A: Yes. All branches have generators and UPS. During outages over 2 hours, the AC runs at reduced power.

**Q: Any tips for training while fasting?**
A: Keep the day session light: walking, mobility, technique. Save heavy lifting and HIIT for the night session after iftar. Nutritionist **Kamran Baig** makes sehri and iftar meal plans (PKR 4,000, or included monthly on Elite).
