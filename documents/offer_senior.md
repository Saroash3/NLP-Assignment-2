# Senior Citizen Discount (Age 60+)

Staying active matters at every age. Strength training helps with balance, bone density and independence. Members aged **60 and above** get **20% off the Standard plan** at Zor Fitness Club.

## Senior prices
| Branch | Normal Standard | Senior price |
|---|---|---|
| F-7 Islamabad | PKR 12,000 | **PKR 9,600** per month |
| Bahria Town Phase 7 | PKR 12,000 | **PKR 9,600** per month |
| G-11 Islamabad | PKR 8,000 | **PKR 6,400** per month |

The senior discount applies to the **Standard plan only**, on monthly or prepaid terms. It is used instead of the prepaid discount, not on top of it. The admission fee is waived on 6-month and 12-month terms as usual.

## Free extras for seniors
- A **free physiotherapy screening** with **Dr. Ayesha Malik** at joining. Normally an assessment costs PKR 3,500. She checks joints, posture and balance, and suggests safe exercise limits.
- A trainer-led orientation focused on **joint-friendly machines**, safe cardio, and getting on and off equipment safely
- Physio follow-ups afterwards get the normal member discount: 20% off PKR 2,500.

## Requirements
- A **CNIC showing age 60+**
- The **PAR-Q health form**. Please declare heart conditions, blood pressure, diabetes, joint replacements, or a history of falls.
- A **doctor's note** before starting if you have a heart condition, or if the physio screening recommends one
- Members with a heart condition also need a doctor's note before joining **HIIT Blast** or **Spin**

## Suggested classes (drop-in PKR 1,000 each on Standard)
- **Strength Foundations**, F-7 main floor, Mon/Wed 7:00–8:00pm, with Head Coach Hamza Qureshi. It is a beginner class and focuses on technique.
- **Yoga Flow (Ladies)**, F-7 Ladies Floor, Tue/Thu 10:00–11:00am, with Mahnoor Rizvi. Good for flexibility and balance.
- **Pilates (Ladies)**, Bahria Ladies Wing, Mon/Wed 10:00–11:00am
- **Ladies Stretch & Mobility**, G-11, Saturday 12:00–12:45pm, with Hira Saleem

## Good times to visit
Mid-morning (roughly 10:00am–4:00pm on weekdays) is the quietest time at F-7 and Bahria. There are no queues for machines, and the peak-time rules (a 20-minute cardio limit 6:00–9:00pm) don't matter.

## Safety
All staff are first-aid trained, and every branch has an AED (defibrillator). The steam room is limited to 15 minutes per session. Seniors with blood pressure problems should ask their doctor before using the steam or sauna.

The senior discount cannot be combined with any other offer.
