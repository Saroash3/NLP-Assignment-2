# Trainer Profile: Usman Tariq

**Role:** Functional Training Coach, Kettlebell Specialist
**Branch:** G-11 Islamabad (men's hours)
**Experience:** 6 years
**Languages:** Urdu, English

## About Usman
Usman Tariq created **Zor Circuit**, the functional training class that has become the signature workout of our compact G-11 branch. With 6 years of experience and a specialisation in **kettlebell training**, Usman is good at getting a full-body workout out of a small space. That skill matters at G-11, which has a single floor, 2 power racks and one studio. He has a friendly, encouraging style and is popular with students and office workers who train before the day starts.

## Class Usman teaches
| Class | Days | Time | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zor Circuit | Mon, Wed, Fri | 7:00–7:45am | G-11 studio (men's hours) |
| **Ramadan:** Zor Circuit | Mon, Wed, Fri | 10:00–10:45pm | G-11 |

Zor Circuit is 45 minutes of station work: kettlebell swings, goblet squats, farmer's carries, sled pushes, box step-ups and core finishers, rotating every 45 seconds. It is all levels, with capacity 15. The 7:00am slot falls inside the student off-peak window (Mon–Fri 6:00am–4:00pm), so many university students attend.

## Personal training availability
- **Monday to Saturday, 6:00–9:30am and 5:00–8:00pm** at G-11
- Sunday: G-11 is closed

PT prices are the same for all trainers: **8 sessions PKR 18,000, 12 sessions PKR 25,000, 20 sessions PKR 38,000**, valid for 60 days from purchase. Usman's PT clients often want fat loss, general fitness, or kettlebell technique (the swing, the clean, and the Turkish get-up).

## Coaching approach
- **Movement quality first:** he teaches the hip hinge before anyone swings a heavy kettlebell
- **Progress tracking:** he logs your circuit scores in the Zor Fitness app so you can see improvement month to month
- **Efficient sessions:** his PT sessions are designed to work even when the floor is busy at peak time, when the 30-minute rack limit applies

## Who should train with Usman?
- Beginners who want an all-round fitness routine without complicated machines
- Busy professionals with only 45 minutes before work
- Students on a budget. Standard at G-11 costs PKR 8,000 per month, or PKR 6,000 with the student discount.
- Members wanting to learn kettlebells properly
