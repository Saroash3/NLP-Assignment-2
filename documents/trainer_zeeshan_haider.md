# Trainer Profile: Zeeshan Haider

**Role:** Spin and Cardio / Endurance Coach
**Branch:** Bahria Town Phase 7, Rawalpindi (spin studio)
**Background:** Endurance athlete. He has completed the Lahore Marathon 3 times.
**Languages:** Urdu, English

## About Zeeshan
Zeeshan Haider runs the **24-bike spin studio** at our Bahria branch, which is Zor Fitness Club's most popular class space. A long-distance runner and cyclist, he has completed the **Lahore Marathon three times** and coaches members who want better stamina, heart health and fat loss. His classes are built around music and heart-rate zones. He explains when to push and when to recover, so beginners and experienced riders can share the same room.

## How to get a bike
Spin classes fill quickly, especially Spin Night on Tuesday and Thursday evenings:
- Booking opens **48 hours ahead**, or **72 hours for Elite** members
- Capacity is **24**, one per bike
- Join the waitlist if it's full. You'll get a WhatsApp message if you're auto-booked.
- Arrive 5 minutes early to set your saddle height. The door closes 10 minutes after the start.

## Health and safety
- Members with a **heart condition** need a **doctor's note** before any Spin class
- Normal trainers are fine, and no cleats are needed. **Closed sports shoes are required.**
- A **towel is mandatory**, and you'll need it
- Bring your own water bottle. There are no plastic cups at the dispensers.

## Who should train with Zeeshan?
- Members wanting low-impact cardio that's easy on the knees
- Runners preparing for a race
- Anyone trying to improve stamina for sports like football or cricket
- Early risers. The 6:30am Spin fits inside the student off-peak window (Mon–Fri 6:00am–4:00pm).

Plus and Elite members book for free. Standard members and non-members pay a PKR 1,000 drop-in. The spin studio is on the main floor side, so Ladies Only members can't book spin classes.
