# Code of Conduct and Privacy Policy

Zor Fitness Club is a respectful space for everyone. Breaking these rules can lead to a warning, suspension, or termination.

## Zero tolerance: immediate termination, no refund
- **Harassment** of any kind: staring, following, unwanted comments, messages, or touching. This covers members, guests and staff.
- **Filming or photographing other people** without consent, and **any filming on the Ladies Floor, in the Ladies Wing or in changing rooms**
- **Violence or threats**
- **Coaching other members for money** (freelance training)
- **Selling supplements, steroids, services or products** on the premises
- A **second tailgating** offence

Terminated members lose the rest of their membership with **no refund**, and may be banned from all three branches.

## Warnings first
These lead to a **written warning**. A second offence may mean suspension or termination:
- The **first tailgating** offence (letting someone in on your fingerprint)
- Repeatedly ignoring floor rules: peak-time equipment limits, re-racking, phone calls on the floor, dropping dumbbells
- Repeated dress-code refusals
- Abusive or loud language, or arguing with staff

## Smoking and substances
- **No smoking, vaping or naswar** anywhere on the premises, including stairwells, parking areas and the entrance
- Alcohol and drugs are strictly prohibited. Members under the influence will be asked to leave.

## Noise and music
- **No speakers**. Use headphones.
- **Phone calls on the gym floor are not allowed**. Use the lobby.
- Reasonable grunting on heavy lifts is fine, but shouting and swearing are not

## Photography and filming rules
- You **may film yourself** on the main floors (F-7, Bahria and G-11 men's hours) for form checks or social media, as long as **nobody else is identifiable** in the frame
- **Never** film on the **Ladies Floor**, in the **Ladies Wing**, during **G-11 ladies hours**, in **changing rooms**, in the **pool** area or in the **steam/sauna**. This applies to everyone, including women filming themselves.
- Staff may ask you to delete a video

## Privacy of your data
- Your CNIC, fingerprint template, health (PAR-Q) form and InBody results are stored securely and used only for membership, entry and safety
- Trainers see your PAR-Q and InBody data only for coaching. Sana Iftikhar and Kamran Baig share notes only with your permission.
- We never sell your data. Corporate members' HR departments receive only billing information, not health data.
- CCTV covers entrances, main floors and parking. There is **no CCTV in changing rooms**, and the Ladies Floor and Wing cameras are monitored only by female staff.

## Religious respect
Separate prayer rooms (F-7, Bahria) and a prayer area (G-11) are available all day. The club closes 1:00pm–2:15pm on Fridays for Jumu'ah. Please keep prayer spaces clean and quiet.

## Reporting a problem
Tell any staff member or the branch manager immediately. Harassment reports are handled confidentially by a female manager if you prefer. You can also email complaints@zorfitness.pk.
