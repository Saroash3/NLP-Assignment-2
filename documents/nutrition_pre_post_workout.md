# What to Eat Before and After a Workout

*General guidance from the Zor Fitness Club team. It is not medical advice. For a plan built around your own goals and routine, book nutritionist Kamran Baig (PKR 4,000, or included once a month on Elite).*

## Before your workout
The goal is **energy without feeling heavy**.

**1–2 hours before training**, a small meal with carbs and a little protein:
- A roti with an egg or a small bowl of daal
- Oats (daliya) with milk and a banana
- Dahi with fruit
- A chicken or egg sandwich on whole-wheat bread

**30–45 minutes before**, if you're short on time, a light snack:
- A banana, or 2–3 dates
- A small handful of roasted chanay
- A **banana-date shake** from the Zor Juice Bar or Café (PKR 550)
- **Black coffee** (PKR 350 at the Juice Bar or Café) if you like a caffeine boost. Keep sugar low.

**Avoid right before training:** oily food (parathas, pakoras, nihari), big meals, and fizzy drinks. They can cause cramps and heaviness, especially in HIIT Blast or Spin.

## Early-morning trainers
If you train at 6:00am (our F-7 and Bahria main floors open then, and Spin starts at 6:30am), you may not want a full meal. A banana or a few dates and water is usually enough. Eat a proper breakfast afterwards.

## After your workout
The goal is **protein for recovery and carbs to refuel**, within about 1–2 hours.
- **A normal desi meal works well:** chicken salan with roti and salad, or daal chawal with dahi
- **Quick options:** 2 boiled eggs (PKR 200 at the Café), a chicken wrap (PKR 750), a glass of milk, or a **whey protein shake** (PKR 850 at the Juice Bar)
- **Rehydrate:** water first. After a sweaty summer session, shikanjabeen with a pinch of salt helps (PKR 250 at the Juice Bar).

## Do I need protein powder?
No, not necessarily. Most people can get enough protein from food: eggs, chicken, daal, dahi and milk. A shake is simply **convenient**. If you buy protein powder, choose a reputable brand and read the label. Remember:
- **Selling supplements on Zor premises is prohibited**, so don't buy from someone on the gym floor
- **Don't mix protein powder at the water dispensers**. Use the changing-room sink.
- Trainers like Ali Raza will **not** advise on steroids or unregulated supplements

## Muscle gain vs fat loss
- **Muscle gain:** eat enough overall, with protein at every meal and a post-workout meal every training day. Ali Raza's Muscle Building Clinic (Fri 6:00–7:00pm, F-7) covers meal timing basics.
- **Fat loss:** keep protein high, reduce oil, sugar and fried snacks, and don't "reward" a workout with mithai. Hira Saleem and Sana Iftikhar pair training with InBody tracking.

## Water
Drink regularly through the day and during training. Bring your own bottle, because there are no plastic cups at our dispensers.
