**Role:** Yoga, Pilates and Prenatal Instructor
**Branches:** F-7 Ladies Floor (Tue/Thu/Sat) and Bahria Ladies Wing (Mon/Wed)
**Qualifications:** 500-hour certified yoga teacher; certified prenatal exercise instructor
**Languages:** Urdu, English

## About Mahnoor
Mahnoor Rizvi teaches the mind-body side of Zor Fitness Club. She trained as a yoga teacher to the 500-hour level and later qualified as a prenatal instructor after many members asked for safe exercise during pregnancy. Her classes are calm, detailed and adaptable. She uses blocks, bolsters and chairs so that every body type and fitness level can take part. She splits her week between our two ladies areas: the F-7 Ladies Floor and the Bahria Ladies Wing.

PT packages cost the same as for all trainers: **8 sessions PKR 18,000 · 12 sessions PKR 25,000 · 20 sessions PKR 38,000**, valid for 60 days. Private sessions are popular for back pain, posture correction and pregnancy.

## Coaching style
Mahnoor focuses on breathing, alignment and slow control rather than extreme flexibility. In Pilates she emphasises core strength and posture for office workers. In prenatal classes she modifies every pose by trimester: no lying flat on the back after the first trimester, and no deep twists or inversions.

## Who should train with Mahnoor?
- Beginners who find the gym floor intimidating
- Women with lower back pain or poor posture from desk jobs (after checking with a doctor or with Dr. Ayesha Malik for pain)
- Expecting mothers, with a doctor's clearance
- New mothers rebuilding core strength after the postpartum check-up
- Runners and lifters who want better mobility
