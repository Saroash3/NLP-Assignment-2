# Training Safely in Summer Heat and Staying Hydrated

*General guidance from the Zor Fitness Club team. It is not medical advice.*

Summers in Islamabad and Rawalpindi can be very hot, especially in June, and the monsoon brings high humidity. Training in heat puts extra strain on your body. Here's how to stay safe.

## Why heat matters
When it's hot, your body sweats more to cool down. You lose water and salts (electrolytes) faster, and your heart works harder. Dehydration can cause headaches, dizziness, cramps and poor performance. In serious cases it leads to heat exhaustion.

## Hydration basics
- **Drink regularly through the day**, not just at the gym. If your urine is dark yellow, you need more water.
- **Before training:** a glass or two of water in the hour before
- **During training:** sip water between sets and after every few minutes of cardio
- **After training:** replace what you lost. After a very sweaty session, a drink with a little salt and sugar helps, such as **shikanjabeen** (PKR 250 at the Zor Juice Bar) or ORS.
- **Bring your own bottle.** Our water dispensers are on every floor, but there are **no plastic cups**.

## Load-shedding and the AC
All Zor branches have **generators and UPS**, so the gym stays open during load-shedding. However, during outages **longer than 2 hours**, the **air-conditioning runs at reduced power** to save fuel. On those days:
- Lower your intensity. It's not the day for a new personal best.
- Take longer rests between sets
- Drink more water
- Choose the lower-impact options in HIIT Blast and Spin

## Best times to train in summer
- **Early morning** (our F-7 and Bahria main floors open at 6:00am) is the coolest time
- **Late evening.** Bahria's main floor stays open until midnight, Mon–Sat.
- Avoid heavy outdoor running at midday

## Steam and sauna in summer
Limit steam and sauna in very hot weather, especially after an intense workout. The rule is a **maximum of 15 minutes**, and drink water before and after. Don't use them if you already feel dizzy or dehydrated.

## Warning signs of heat exhaustion
- Heavy sweating, pale or clammy skin
- Dizziness, nausea or a headache
- Muscle cramps
- A fast, weak pulse

**If you feel any of these:** stop, move to a cool spot, drink water, and tell a staff member. All staff are first-aid trained. If symptoms don't improve quickly, get medical help. Call Rescue 1122 in an emergency.

## Clothing
Light, breathable athletic wear. Remember that closed sports shoes and a towel are required on the gym floor. In the monsoon, carry a separate pair of gym shoes to keep the floor clean.

## Fasting in summer
When Ramadan falls in summer, fasting days are long and hot. Keep day-session training very light and train properly only in the night session after iftar. See our sehri and iftar guide.
