# Refund Policy

**General rule:** Zor Fitness Club membership fees are **non-refundable**. There are only three exceptions, listed below. Please read this before buying a prepaid plan.

## Exception 1: 7-day cooling-off
- You can get a **full refund of the plan fee** if you ask within **7 days of joining**
- **Condition:** you must not have visited even once. We check the **biometric (fingerprint) entry log**. A single entry, even just for the orientation or an InBody scan, cancels the cooling-off right.
- The **PKR 10,000 admission fee is NOT refunded**, even under cooling-off
- It applies to every plan and term, including monthly plans

## Exception 2: Relocation
- **Only for 6-month and 12-month plans**
- You must be moving **more than 50 km from every Zor branch**, for example from Islamabad to Lahore or Karachi. A move within the twin cities or to Taxila doesn't qualify.
- **Proof of the new address** is required: a utility bill in your name, a rental agreement, or an employer's transfer letter
- **Refund amount:** the pro-rata value of the **unused full months only** (part months are not refunded), **minus a 10% processing fee**
- Example: 12-month Plus (PKR 144,000 = PKR 12,000 per month on the prepaid rate). If you leave with 4 full months unused, 4 × PKR 12,000 = PKR 48,000, minus 10% = PKR 43,200 refunded.

## Exception 3: Permanent medical inability
- **Only for 6-month and 12-month plans**
- A doctor's certificate must state that you are **permanently** unable to exercise
- It is calculated the same way as relocation: unused full months minus 10%
- For **temporary** conditions such as surgery recovery or pregnancy, use the **medical freeze** (up to 60 days, free) instead of a refund

## Never refunded
- The admission fee
- **PT packages**, even if unused within their 60-day validity
- Day passes (PKR 1,500), drop-in classes (PKR 1,000) and pool drop-ins (PKR 1,500)
- Café and Juice Bar purchases, towel rental, and padlocks
- Days lost to an **instalment suspension**
- Memberships **terminated for misconduct** (harassment, tailgating on a second offence, or coaching for money)
- Monthly plans after the 7-day cooling-off. Just don't renew them.

## How refunds are paid
- Refunds are processed within **14 working days**
- They go back to the **original payment method**. **Cash payments are refunded by bank transfer**, so bring your IBAN.
- If you paid a 12-month plan in instalments, the refund is based on what you actually paid

## How to request a refund
Fill in the refund form at your branch front desk, or email hello@zorfitness.pk with your member number, the reason, and your proof. If you disagree with the decision, use the complaints process: the branch manager, then complaints@zorfitness.pk.
