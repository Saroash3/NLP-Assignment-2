# Trainer Profile: Hira Saleem

**Role:** Ladies Functional Training and Weight-Loss Coach
**Branch:** G-11 Islamabad (ladies hours)
**Qualifications:** Certified personal trainer
**Languages:** Urdu, English

## About Hira
Hira Saleem leads women's training at our compact **G-11 branch**, where the single floor becomes women-only from **10:30am to 4:30pm, Monday to Saturday**. She is a certified personal trainer who specialises in **weight loss and functional strength for women**, particularly mothers returning to exercise and university students. Hira runs the ladies version of Zor Circuit and the gentle Saturday Stretch & Mobility class. Members describe her as practical and motivating.

## Classes Hira teaches
| Class | Days | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Ladies Zor Circuit | Tue, Thu | 11:00–11:45am |
| Ladies Stretch & Mobility | Sat | 12:00–12:45pm |

Both are all levels, capacity 15, in the G-11 studio during ladies hours. Neither runs in Ramadan. The only Ramadan class at G-11 is Usman Tariq's men's Zor Circuit.

## Personal training availability
- **Monday to Saturday, 10:30am–4:00pm** at G-11
- Not available on Sunday, when G-11 is closed

PT packages are the same price for all trainers: **8 sessions PKR 18,000 · 12 sessions PKR 25,000 · 20 sessions PKR 38,000**, valid for 60 days and non-refundable. Hira's typical PT client wants to lose 5–10 kg, feel stronger, and build a routine that fits around school timings or university lectures.

## Her weight-loss approach
- **Strength training 2–3 times a week**, not just cardio
- **Daily step targets** tracked in the app
- **InBody scans** to track fat loss and muscle, not just weight on a scale. Plus, Elite and Ladies Only get 1 per month, and Standard 1 per quarter.
- For diet, she refers members to nutritionist **Kamran Baig** (PKR 4,000 per consultation, or included once a month on Elite). Hira gives general healthy-eating tips but does not write meal plans.

## Who should train with Hira?
- Women who prefer a quiet, women-only session near G-10, G-11 or G-13
- Mothers who train between school drop-off and pick-up
- Students on the student discount. Their off-peak window (Mon–Fri 6:00am–4:00pm) overlaps with ladies hours from 10:30am to 4:00pm.
- Beginners who want to learn kettlebells and dumbbells in a supportive group
