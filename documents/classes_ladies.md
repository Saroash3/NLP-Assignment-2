# Ladies Classes: PCOS Program, Yoga Flow, Zumba and Pilates

These classes are **for women only** and are taught by **female trainers** on the F-7 Ladies Floor and the Bahria Ladies Wing.

## Ladies Strength & PCOS Program
- **When:** Monday, Wednesday, Friday, **11:00am–12:00pm**, F-7 Ladies Floor
- **Coach:** **Sana Iftikhar**, a women's strength coach with 7 years' experience and a former national-level netball player
- **Level:** all levels. Capacity 15.
- Strength training designed for women with PCOS, insulin resistance or hormonal weight gain: resistance exercise, low-impact conditioning, and education on consistency. Sana works alongside nutritionist **Kamran Baig** for members who want a diet plan. This is general fitness support, not medical treatment, so please follow your doctor's advice.

## Yoga Flow (Ladies)
- **When:** Tuesday and Thursday, **10:00–11:00am**, F-7 Ladies Floor (in Ramadan: Tue/Thu 9:30–10:15pm)
- **Coach:** **Mahnoor Rizvi**, a 500-hour certified yoga teacher
- **Level:** all levels. Mats are provided. It is a vinyasa-style flow focused on flexibility, breathing and stress relief.

## Zumba (Ladies)
- **F-7 Ladies Floor:** Monday and Wednesday, **5:00–6:00pm**
- **Bahria Ladies Wing:** Tuesday and Thursday, **4:00–5:00pm** (in Ramadan: Tue/Thu 9:30–10:15pm)
- **Coach:** **Farah Naz**, a licensed Zumba instructor since 2018
- **Capacity:** 25, our biggest class size. It is dance fitness to Latin, Bollywood and Punjabi music, with no dance experience needed.

## Pilates (Ladies)
- **When:** Monday and Wednesday, **10:00–11:00am**, Bahria Ladies Wing
- **Coach:** Mahnoor Rizvi
- Mat Pilates for core strength, posture and back health. It is suitable for beginners and for recovery after childbirth, once a doctor has cleared exercise.

## Also for women
- **Prenatal Yoga**, Saturday 11:00am–12:00pm, F-7 Ladies Floor (doctor's clearance required). See the Prenatal Yoga document.
- **Ladies Zor Circuit** (Tue/Thu 11:00–11:45am) and **Ladies Stretch & Mobility** (Sat 12:00–12:45pm) at G-11 with Hira Saleem

## Who can join?
- **Ladies Only, Plus and Elite** (female members): all ladies classes, free
- **Standard** female members: PKR 1,000 drop-in at their home branch
- Book in the app or on WhatsApp up to 48 hours ahead (Elite 72 hours). Cancel at least 3 hours before.

## Dress and privacy
- Any athletic wear. **Sports hijab and full-length athletic wear are welcome.**
- In the yoga and Pilates studios, **shalwar kameez is allowed** and you can go barefoot on the mat. Closed sports shoes are required for Zumba and strength.
- **No photography or filming** on the Ladies Floor or in the Ladies Wing.
- Mothers can use the free **kids' play area** at F-7 (ages 3–8, 10:00am–4:00pm, maximum 90 minutes) during classes there.
