# About Zor Fitness Club and How to Contact Us

## Our story
**Zor Fitness Club** ("zor" means strength) was founded in **2019** in Islamabad. We started with one branch in **F-7 Markaz**, opened a compact branch in **G-11 Markaz** in **2021**, and our third branch in **Bahria Town Phase 7, Rawalpindi** in **2023**. We aim to be a serious, clean and respectful gym for the twin cities, with proper women's facilities, qualified coaches, and fair, transparent pricing.

## What we offer
- 4 plans: **Standard** (PKR 12,000, or PKR 8,000 at G-11), **Plus** (PKR 16,000), **Elite** (PKR 26,000) and **Ladies Only** (PKR 10,000)
- 17 weekly group classes across the branches, from powerlifting to Zumba
- 9 coaches, plus a physiotherapist (Dr. Ayesha Malik) and a registered dietitian (Kamran Baig)
- A 20m heated pool (F-7), a 24-bike spin studio and boxing zone (Bahria), steam at F-7 and Bahria, and sauna at F-7

## Our values
- **Respect:** harassment means immediate termination with no refund. There is no filming on the Ladies Floor or Ladies Wing.
- **Honest pricing:** there is no auto-renewal. You renew yourself, and the app reminds you 7 days before.
- **Qualified coaching only:** outside or freelance trainers are not allowed on our floors.
- **Community:** we close for Jumu'ah every Friday (1:00pm–2:15pm) and adjust our hours for Ramadan.

## What we don't offer
- **Home training.** All training is at our branches.
- **Cheque payments.** We accept cash, card, bank transfer, JazzCash and Easypaisa.
- Selling supplements on the premises. Neither staff nor members may sell supplements or services inside the club.

## Social and updates
Branch notices (Eid closures, class changes, Ramadan timetables) are posted in the app and sent on WhatsApp. For urgent issues during opening hours, speak to the branch manager at the front desk.
