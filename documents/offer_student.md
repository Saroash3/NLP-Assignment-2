# Student Discount

Zor Fitness Club gives students **25% off the Standard or Plus plan**, on monthly or prepaid terms. It helps students from schools, colleges and universities around Islamabad and Rawalpindi train affordably.

## Who qualifies
- Aged **16 to 25**
- A **valid student ID** from a Pakistani school, college or university. Matric, FSc/A-levels, undergraduate and postgraduate are all accepted.
- Members aged 16–17 also need a parent's consent signature and their B-form
- Your student ID is **rechecked every 6 months**. If you can't show a valid ID at the recheck, you move to the normal price at your next renewal.

## Student prices (per month)
| Plan | Normal | Student |
|---|---|---|
| Standard, G-11 | PKR 8,000 | PKR 6,000 |
| Standard, F-7 or Bahria | PKR 12,000 | PKR 9,000 |
| Plus | PKR 16,000 | PKR 12,000 |

The 25% student discount is applied **instead of** the prepaid discount if you buy 3, 6 or 12 months. You don't get both. The admission fee (PKR 10,000) is still waived on 6-month and 12-month terms.

## The off-peak rule
Student members can enter **only during off-peak hours: Monday to Friday, 6:00am–4:00pm**.
- Your fingerprint won't open the door in the evenings or at weekends
- Female students training on the ladies floor or wing, or during G-11 ladies hours, can train within the ladies hours that fall inside this window. For example, at G-11 that is 10:30am–4:00pm.
- Evening classes like Strength Foundations (7:00–8:00pm) or Spin Night are outside student hours. Students on Plus can book morning and daytime classes such as Spin (6:30–7:15am), Zor Circuit (7:00–7:45am) and ladies daytime classes.

## Student exam freeze
Once a year, student members on a **3-month or longer** plan can freeze for **30 days free** during exams. You need to show your official **date sheet**. This is **in addition to** the normal freeze allowance (7, 15 or 30 days depending on plan length). Request it 48 hours ahead at the desk, in the app or on WhatsApp.

## Not available on
- Elite or Ladies Only plans
- Together with any other discount (family, couple, corporate, senior or Ramadan Fit Pass). If you qualify for two, you choose one.

## Tip for university students
Many students choose Standard at G-11 for PKR 6,000 a month and train between morning lectures. If you have evening classes at university, the off-peak window usually fits well.
