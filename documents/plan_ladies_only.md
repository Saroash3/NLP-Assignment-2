# Ladies Only Membership Plan

Many of our members prefer to train in a women-only space with female trainers and staff. The Ladies Only plan gives access to the women's areas at **all three** Zor Fitness Club branches for one price.

## Price
**PKR 10,000 per month** at all branches.

| Term | Price |
|---|---|
| 1 month | PKR 10,000 (+ PKR 10,000 admission) |
| 3 months | PKR 27,000 (+ PKR 10,000 admission) |
| 6 months | PKR 51,000 (admission waived) |
| 12 months | PKR 90,000 (admission waived) |

## Where and when you can train
- **F-7 Islamabad – Ladies Floor.** This is on the 2nd floor, with a separate side entrance on College Road. Open Mon–Sat 7:00am–10:00pm and Sunday 10:00am–6:00pm.
- **Bahria Town Phase 7 – Ladies Wing.** This is on the ground floor, with a separate rear entrance. Open Mon–Sat 8:00am–9:00pm and **closed on Sunday**.
- **G-11 Islamabad – ladies hours.** The single floor is reserved for women Mon–Sat 10:30am–4:30pm. G-11 is **closed on Sunday**.
- Last entry is 45 minutes before closing.

## What's included
- **All ladies classes**: Ladies Strength & PCOS Program, Yoga Flow and Zumba at F-7; Prenatal Yoga at F-7 on Saturdays (doctor's clearance needed); Pilates and Zumba at Bahria; Ladies Zor Circuit and Ladies Stretch & Mobility at G-11
- **Ladies steam room** at F-7 and Bahria, plus the ladies sauna at F-7
- The **ladies pool session** at F-7, Mon–Sat 11:00am–3:00pm, with a female lifeguard, and the Sunday family swim 10:00am–2:00pm
- One InBody scan per month
- Female trainers and front-desk staff throughout the women's areas

## Kids' play area
Mothers training on the F-7 Ladies Floor can use the free supervised kids' play area for children aged 3–8, from 10:00am to 4:00pm, for a maximum of 90 minutes per visit. This is available to **any female member on any plan**, not just Ladies Only. There is no kids' area at G-11 or Bahria.

## Privacy rules
- Photography and filming by anyone are **not allowed** on the Ladies Floor or Ladies Wing, or in the changing rooms.
- Guests you bring must be female, and pay PKR 1,000 per visit.
- Male trainers do not coach on the Ladies Floor or Ladies Wing.

## Ladies Only vs other plans
Ladies Only covers only the women's areas. A woman who also wants the main floor (for example, to use the F-7 deadlift platform or the Bahria spin studio) should choose Standard, Plus or Elite. Female members on those plans can use both the main floor and the ladies areas. The student discount does not apply to Ladies Only, but family, corporate and Ramadan offers do.
