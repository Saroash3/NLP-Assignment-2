# Complaints and Feedback Policy

We want every member to have a good experience at Zor Fitness Club. If something goes wrong, here's how to raise it and what to expect.

## Step 1: Speak to the branch manager
Most issues are fixed fastest on the spot. Ask the front desk for the **branch manager** at F-7, G-11 or Bahria. Typical issues solved at this stage:
- Broken equipment or a machine left out of order
- Cleanliness in changing rooms
- A class cancelled without notice
- Disputes over peak-time equipment limits
- Billing mistakes on a receipt

## Step 2: Email the complaints team
If the branch manager can't resolve it, or you'd rather put it in writing, email **complaints@zorfitness.pk** with:
- Your name and member number
- The branch, date and time
- What happened, with photos or receipts if relevant. Please don't send photos taken on the Ladies Floor or in the Ladies Wing, because filming there isn't allowed.
- What outcome you want

**You'll get a reply within 3 working days.**

## Step 3: Operations manager
If you're still not satisfied, ask for your complaint to be **escalated to the operations manager**. You'll get a final response **within 7 working days**.

## Sensitive complaints
- **Harassment** reports can be made directly to any staff member, and are handled confidentially
- Female members can ask for their report to be handled by a **female manager**
- Harassment by a member leads to **immediate termination without refund** once confirmed
- Complaints about a trainer are reviewed by the Head Coach, **Hamza Qureshi**, together with the branch manager. Complaints about the Head Coach go to the operations manager.

## Refund disputes
If your refund request (cooling-off, relocation or medical) was rejected and you disagree, follow the same steps. Remember that refunds are only possible under those three exceptions, and the admission fee is never refunded.

## General feedback and suggestions
Not a complaint? We love ideas: new class times, equipment requests, café menu suggestions. Share them:
- In the **Zor Fitness app** (Profile → Feedback)
- On **WhatsApp 0333-5550967**
- By email: **hello@zorfitness.pk**
- On the suggestion box at each front desk

Popular suggestions from members led to the Sunday Long Ride at Bahria and Ladies Stretch & Mobility at G-11.

## What we ask of members
- Stay calm and respectful with staff. Abusive language towards staff is itself a conduct violation.
- Give us the chance to fix the problem before posting publicly
- Keep receipts. All payments generate receipts in the app.
