# Bahria Town Phase 7 Rawalpindi Branch

**Address:** Plot 3, Spring North Commercial, Bahria Town Phase 7, Rawalpindi
**Opened:** 2023
**Ladies entrance:** a separate rear entrance leading to the ground-floor Ladies Wing

Bahria is our newest branch. It has the **24-bike spin studio**, a **boxing zone with a ring**, and the latest closing time of any Zor branch: **midnight**, Monday to Saturday.

## Opening hours
| Area | Mon–Sat | Sunday |
|---|---|---|
| Main floor (men / mixed plans) | 6:00am–12:00am (midnight) | 10:00am–8:00pm |
| Ladies Wing | 8:00am–9:00pm | **Closed** |

**On Sunday the Bahria main floor is OPEN 10:00am–8:00pm; only the Ladies Wing is closed on Sunday.**

Last entry is 45 minutes before closing. The branch closes on Fridays 1:00pm–2:15pm for Jumu'ah.

## Facilities
- **Main floor:** cardio, free weights, machines and **4 power racks**
- **Spin studio** with 24 bikes. It is booked out most evenings, so Elite members' 72-hour booking window helps here.
- **Boxing zone:** 4 heavy bags and a ring, used for HIIT Blast and open boxing practice
- **Ladies Wing:** full equipment, a studio for Pilates and Zumba, and the ladies steam room
- **Steam rooms** for men and ladies. **There is no sauna at Bahria.** Sauna is only at F-7.
- **No pool.** The pool is at F-7.
- **Zor Café** (card or JazzCash only; members get 10% off), prayer rooms, and **free open parking**

## Price at Bahria
Standard with Bahria as home branch costs PKR 12,000 per month, the same as F-7. Plus, Elite and Ladies Only include Bahria.

## Who uses Bahria?
Residents of Bahria Town, DHA Phase 2, and nearby Rawalpindi societies. Plus members often combine Bahria on weekends (Sunday opening from 10:00am) with F-7 or G-11 on weekdays near their Islamabad offices. Because the Ladies Wing is closed on Sundays, women who want to train on Sunday can use the F-7 Ladies Floor (10:00am–6:00pm) if their plan covers F-7.
