# Admission (Joining) Fee

Every new member of Zor Fitness Club pays a **one-time admission fee of PKR 10,000** when they join, unless one of the waivers below applies.

## What the admission fee covers
- Registering your membership and your CNIC details
- Biometric (fingerprint) enrolment for entry at the branches your plan covers
- Your member card. A replacement costs PKR 1,000 if it is lost.
- The PAR-Q health questionnaire review
- A free 30-minute floor orientation with a trainer
- Your first InBody body-composition scan

## When the admission fee is waived
1. **6-month or 12-month prepaid plans:** no admission fee on any plan type.
2. **Corporate members:** all employees joining through a company agreement (5 or more employees).
3. **Ramadan Fit Pass:** new members joining during Ramadan pay PKR 11,000 for one month of Plus and no admission fee. If they continue on any plan right after the pass ends, they still don't pay it.
4. **Families:** when 3 or more members of the same household join, only **one** admission fee is charged for the whole family.

It is **not** waived for 1-month or 3-month plans, student members, couples, or seniors, unless they choose a 6-month or 12-month plan.

## When you have to pay it again
The admission fee is normally paid only once. However:
- When your plan expires, you have a **5-day grace period** to renew with no penalty.
- After that your account becomes **inactive**. You can still renew at the normal price within **60 days**.
- If you have been inactive for **more than 60 days**, you rejoin as a new member and pay the PKR 10,000 admission fee again, unless you choose a 6 or 12-month plan.

Freezes do not count as inactive time. A frozen member keeps their status.

## Refunds
The admission fee is **never refunded**, in any situation. It is not returned even under the 7-day cooling-off refund, which returns only the plan fee, or under relocation or medical refunds.

## Common questions
- **Is the admission fee different at G-11?** No. It is PKR 10,000 at every branch, even though Standard at G-11 costs only PKR 8,000 a month.
- **Does a day pass or free trial need an admission fee?** No. The free trial day and the PKR 1,500 day pass don't require joining.
- **Can I transfer my admission to my brother?** No. Only a 12-month plan can be transferred, once, to immediate family for PKR 3,000.
