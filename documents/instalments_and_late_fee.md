# Instalments, Late Fee and Payment Methods

## Who can pay in instalments?
Only **12-month plans** can be paid in instalments. Monthly, 3-month and 6-month plans must be paid in full up front.

A 12-month plan is split into **2 equal instalments**:
| Plan | 12-month price | Each instalment |
|---|---|---|
| Standard (F-7 / Bahria) | PKR 108,000 | PKR 54,000 |
| Standard (G-11) | PKR 72,000 | PKR 36,000 |
| Plus | PKR 144,000 | PKR 72,000 |
| Elite | PKR 234,000 | PKR 117,000 |
| Ladies Only | PKR 90,000 | PKR 45,000 |

The first instalment is paid on joining. The **second instalment is due on the first day of month 7**. The app reminds you 7 days before.

## Late fee
- Days 1–7 after the due date: no penalty. Entry continues as normal.
- **After 7 days unpaid: a late fee of PKR 1,500** is added.
- **After 15 days unpaid: entry is suspended** until the second instalment and the late fee are paid.
- **Suspended days are not added back** to your plan. Unlike a freeze, your end date does not move.
- A suspended member cannot freeze their membership or transfer it until the balance is cleared.

## Discounts with instalments
The admission fee is still waived on 12-month plans paid in instalments. Couple, family, corporate and senior discounts are applied to the total price before it is split. Corporate members are invoiced to their company instead, so the instalment option is normally not used.

## Accepted payment methods
- **Cash** at any branch front desk. You get a printed receipt.
- **Debit or credit card**, with **no surcharge**, including for payments over PKR 50,000
- **Bank transfer.** Share the transfer screenshot on WhatsApp (0333-5550967) with your member number.
- **JazzCash** and **Easypaisa**
- **Cheques are not accepted.**
- At the **Zor Juice Bar and Zor Café**, only card or JazzCash is accepted, not cash.

## Refunds of instalments
If you qualify for a refund (the 7-day cooling-off, relocation, or permanent medical inability), the refund is calculated on what you have actually paid. Refunds are sent within 14 working days to the original payment method. Cash payments are refunded by bank transfer.

## Receipts and invoices
Every payment creates a receipt in the Zor Fitness app. Companies on the corporate scheme receive one combined invoice. For any billing question, email hello@zorfitness.pk or call UAN 051-111-967-348 (9:00am–9:00pm daily).
