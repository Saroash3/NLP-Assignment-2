## About Kamran
Kamran Baig is a **registered dietitian** who specialises in practical **desi-diet meal plans**: eating roti, daal, sabzi, chicken salan and chawal in the right amounts, rather than expensive imported "diet food". He also creates **Ramadan sehri and iftar plans**, muscle-gain plans, fat-loss plans, and meal plans that support members with PCOS in the Ladies Strength & PCOS Program run by Sana Iftikhar.

## What happens in a consultation
1. A review of your goals, your daily routine (office hours, school runs, night shifts) and your food preferences
2. Your latest **InBody scan** results. Scans are included in plans, or cost PKR 800 extra.
3. A **7-day meal plan** built from normal Pakistani home food, with portion guides using rotis, cups and your own hand
4. Advice on water intake, protein sources, and eating out at weddings and dawats
5. A follow-up target, usually after 4 weeks

## Kamran's approach
- **No crash diets** and no cutting out roti completely
- **Protein at every meal:** eggs, daal, chana, dahi, chicken, fish
- **Reduce the hidden sugars:** mithai, sugary chai (3–4 cups a day adds up) and cold drinks
- **Budget-friendly:** plans fit a normal household grocery budget

## What Kamran does not do
- He does **not** prescribe medicines or treat medical conditions. Members with diabetes, kidney disease or thyroid problems should follow their doctor's advice. Kamran adjusts meal plans only within that advice.
- He does not sell supplements. Selling supplements on Zor premises is not allowed.
- He does not write workout programmes. That is the trainers' job.
