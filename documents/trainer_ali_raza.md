# Trainer Profile: Ali Raza

**Role:** Bodybuilding and Muscle-Gain Coach
**Branches:** G-11 Islamabad (Mon/Wed/Sat) and F-7 Islamabad (Tue/Thu/Fri)
**Experience:** 9 years; provincial-level bodybuilding competitor
**Languages:** Urdu, English, Punjabi

## About Ali
Ali Raza has 9 years of coaching experience and has **competed in bodybuilding at the provincial level**. He is Zor Fitness Club's go-to coach for members who want to **build muscle**, whether that means skinny beginners trying to gain their first 5 kg or experienced lifters preparing for a physique show. He splits his week between G-11 and F-7, so members at both Islamabad branches can train with him.

## Class Ali teaches

## Ali's muscle-gain principles
1. **Progressive overload:** a little more weight or reps each week, recorded in the app
2. **Enough protein and calories:** for full meal plans he refers members to nutritionist **Kamran Baig**, since Ali is not a dietitian
3. **Sleep and recovery:** 7–8 hours of sleep, and no training the same muscle two days in a row
4. **No shortcuts:** Ali will not advise on steroids or unregulated supplements. Selling supplements on Zor premises is prohibited for staff and members.

## Who should train with Ali?
- "Hard gainers" who struggle to put on weight
- Intermediate lifters wanting a structured bodybuilding split
- Members preparing for a wedding, a photoshoot, or a physique competition
- Evening trainers. All of his slots are after 5:00pm.
