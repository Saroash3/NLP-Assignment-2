# Gym Floor Rules and Etiquette

These rules apply on every Zor Fitness Club gym floor: the F-7 main floor and Ladies Floor, the Bahria main floor and Ladies Wing, and the G-11 floor.

## Peak-time limits (weekdays 6:00–9:00pm)
Weekday evenings are our busiest time. From **6:00 to 9:00pm, Monday to Friday**:
- **Cardio machines** (treadmills, bikes, ellipticals, rowers): a **20-minute limit** when someone is waiting
- **Power racks: a 30-minute limit** when someone is waiting. F-7 has 6 racks, Bahria 4, and G-11 only 2, so please share.
- Don't "reserve" equipment with a towel or bottle while you train elsewhere
- Let others work in between your sets on machines when it's busy
- These limits don't apply to members attending a class inside the class space

## Chalk
- **Only liquid chalk** is allowed. No block or powder chalk.
- Chalk may be used **only on the deadlift platform (F-7) and in the power racks**
- Wipe chalk marks off bars after use

## Phones and music
- **Phone calls are not allowed on the gym floor.** Please take calls in the lobby. You can use your phone for music, workout apps and timers.
- **No speakers.** Use headphones. The club's own music plays through the house system.
- Video calls are not allowed on the floor

## Equipment care
- **Re-rack weights** after use: plates back on the trees and dumbbells in order
- **Wipe down** benches and machines with the spray and paper provided. A towel is mandatory.
- **Controlled drops only.** Drop sets are fine, but **dropping dumbbells from height is not allowed**. Deadlifts can be lowered under control on the platform. Bumper-plate drops are allowed only on the F-7 deadlift platform.
- Report broken equipment at the front desk. Don't try to repair it yourself.

## Water and food
- Water bottles are allowed. There are **no plastic cups** at the dispensers.
- **Don't mix protein powders at the water dispensers**
- No food on the gym floor. Eat at the Juice Bar (F-7) or Café (Bahria).

## Coaching and selling
- **Outside or freelance trainers are not allowed.** A member coaching another member **for money** has their membership **terminated without refund**.
- **Selling supplements, services or products on the premises is prohibited**, whether by staff or members
- Friendly tips between members are fine, but don't give unasked-for advice

## Photos and videos
You may film **yourself only**, and only on the main floors. **Never** film on the Ladies Floor, in the Ladies Wing or in changing rooms, and never film other members without their permission.

## Entry
One person, one fingerprint. **Tailgating** (letting someone enter on your fingerprint) means a written warning the first time and termination the second time.

## Staff authority
Floor staff can ask anyone to follow these rules. Serious or repeated breaches may lead to suspension or termination.
