# G-11 Islamabad Branch (Compact)

**Address:** 2nd floor, Al-Hafeez Plaza, G-11 Markaz, Islamabad
**Opened:** 2021

G-11 is Zor Fitness Club's compact, budget-friendly branch. It serves students and families in G-10, G-11, G-13 and nearby sectors. It has a **single shared floor**, so men and women train at **different times** instead of on separate floors.

## Opening hours (Mon–Sat)
| Time | Who |
|---|---|
| 6:00–10:00am | Men |
| 10:00–10:30am | Floor cleaning (closed) |
| **10:30am–4:30pm** | **Ladies hours** (women only, female staff) |
| 4:30–5:00pm | Floor cleaning (closed) |
| 5:00–11:00pm | Men |

**G-11 is closed every Sunday.** Last entry is 45 minutes before each session closes. On Fridays the floor also closes 1:00pm–2:15pm for Jumu'ah, which falls inside ladies hours.

## Facilities
- Cardio machines, free weights with dumbbells up to **40 kg**, pin machines, and **2 power racks**
- **1 studio** for group classes
- A prayer area
- **No pool, no steam, no sauna.** Members who want those should choose Plus or Elite and use F-7 or Bahria.
- **Street parking only.** There is no building parking.

## Price
**Standard at G-11 costs PKR 8,000 per month**, the cheapest plan at Zor. Prepaid: 3 months PKR 21,600, 6 months PKR 40,800, 12 months PKR 72,000. Students pay PKR 6,000 a month and seniors PKR 6,400. Plus, Elite and Ladies Only members can also use G-11.

## Classes at G-11
- **Zor Circuit** (men's hours): Mon/Wed/Fri 7:00–7:45am, with **Usman Tariq**, kettlebell and functional circuits
- **Ladies Zor Circuit** (ladies hours): Tue/Thu 11:00–11:45am, with **Hira Saleem**
- **Ladies Stretch & Mobility** (ladies hours): Saturday 12:00–12:45pm, with Hira Saleem
- In Ramadan only Zor Circuit runs here, at night: Mon/Wed/Fri 10:00–10:45pm

## G-11-only rules
- **No guests Mon–Fri 6:00–9:00pm.** The compact floor gets too crowded at peak time. Guests are welcome at other times (PKR 1,000 per visit).
- Peak-time limits apply: 20 minutes on cardio machines and 30 minutes on a power rack when someone is waiting. With only 2 racks, please be considerate.
- Standard members whose home branch is G-11 cannot enter F-7 or Bahria. They can change home branch free once per quarter, paying the price difference.

## Staff
**Usman Tariq** (men's hours), **Hira Saleem** (ladies hours), **Ali Raza** (Mon/Wed/Sat, 5:00–10:00pm), and physiotherapist **Dr. Ayesha Malik** on Saturdays 11am–3pm by appointment.
