# Plus Membership Plan

Plus is Zor Fitness Club's most popular plan. It gives you every branch, every group class, and steam and sauna after your workout, at a single price across the twin cities.

## Price
**PKR 16,000 per month**, the same at F-7 Islamabad, G-11 Islamabad and Bahria Town Phase 7 Rawalpindi.

| Term | Price | Admission fee |
|---|---|---|
| 1 month | PKR 16,000 | PKR 10,000 |
| 3 months (10% off) | PKR 43,200 | PKR 10,000 |
| 6 months (15% off) | PKR 81,600 | Waived |
| 12 months (25% off) | PKR 144,000 | Waived |

A 12-month Plus plan can be paid in 2 equal instalments of PKR 72,000. The second one is due on the first day of month 7.

## What's included
- **All 3 branches.** Your fingerprint works at F-7, G-11 and Bahria, so you can train near the office on weekdays and near home on weekends.
- **All group classes**, including Strength Foundations, Powerlifting Club, Boxing Conditioning, Muscle Building Clinic, Zor Circuit, Spin, Spin Night, Sunday Long Ride and HIIT Blast. Female Plus members can also join all ladies classes: Ladies Strength & PCOS Program, Yoga Flow, Prenatal Yoga, Zumba, Pilates, Ladies Zor Circuit, and Ladies Stretch & Mobility.
- **Steam and sauna at F-7**, and the **steam room at Bahria**. Bahria has no sauna, and G-11 has no steam or sauna.
- **One InBody scan every month**, compared with one per quarter on Standard
- Class booking up to **48 hours** ahead in the Zor Fitness app

## What's not included
- **Pool:** Plus members pay **PKR 1,500 per swim** at F-7. Pool access is included only in Elite (and the ladies session in Ladies Only).
- **Personal training:** buy a PT package (8 sessions for PKR 18,000, 12 for PKR 25,000, 20 for PKR 38,000), or upgrade to Elite, which includes 4 sessions a month.
- **Free guest passes:** guests pay PKR 1,000 per visit.
- **Towels:** bring your own, or rent one for PKR 300 per day or PKR 2,000 per month.

## Upgrading to Plus
You can upgrade from Standard to Plus in the middle of a plan by paying the pro-rata difference for the remaining days. Downgrading from Plus to Standard only takes effect at your next renewal.

## Discounts on Plus
- **Student:** 25% off, i.e. PKR 12,000 per month, with off-peak entry only (Mon–Fri 6:00am–4:00pm)
- **Couple:** 10% off each partner, i.e. PKR 14,400 per month each
- **Family** (3+ in one household): 15% off each
- **Corporate:** 15% (5–19 employees) or 20% (20+ employees)
- **Ramadan Fit Pass:** new members only, one month of Plus for PKR 11,000 during Ramadan
- Only one discount can apply at a time.

## Who is Plus for?
Members who want two or more classes a week, or who live in Rawalpindi but work in Islamabad.
