# Strength Classes: Strength Foundations, Powerlifting Club and Muscle Building Clinic

Zor Fitness Club runs three strength classes, all on the **F-7 Islamabad main floor**, where there are 6 power racks and a deadlift platform.

## Strength Foundations
- **When:** Monday and Wednesday, **7:00–8:00pm**
- **Coach:** Head Coach **Hamza Qureshi**
- **Level:** **Beginner.** It is ideal if you've never used a barbell.
- **Capacity:** 15
- **What you'll do:** the five basic movements (squat, hinge/deadlift, push, pull, carry) with light weights and a lot of technique coaching. Each week builds on the last, over an 8-week cycle. You'll learn how to set up in a power rack, how to use safety pins, and how to brace your core.
- **Good for:** new members, women on Plus or Elite who want to learn the main-floor equipment, seniors (with physio clearance), and anyone coming back after injury

## Powerlifting Club
- **When:** Saturday, **9:00–11:00am**. It is 2 hours long, our longest class.
- **Coach:** **Hamza Qureshi**. He has 11 years of coaching experience and a 260 kg deadlift personal record.
- **Level:** **Intermediate and above.** You should be able to squat, bench press and deadlift with good form. Hamza may ask new attendees to take Strength Foundations first.
- **Capacity:** 15
- **What you'll do:** heavy squat, bench press and deadlift in a structured programme with percentages of your max. There are monthly "test days" to set new personal bests, and advice on competing in local powerlifting meets.
- **Rules:** **liquid chalk only**, used on the deadlift platform and the racks only. A lifting belt is allowed, but bring your own. Weights must be stripped and re-racked after your set.

## Muscle Building Clinic
- **When:** Friday, **6:00–7:00pm**
- **Coach:** **Ali Raza**, 9 years' experience and a provincial-level bodybuilding competitor
- **Level:** **Intermediate**
- **Capacity:** 15
- **What you'll do:** hypertrophy training with a different body part focus each week, drop sets, supersets and mind-muscle connection work. The last 10 minutes are a Q&A on training splits and meal timing. For full meal plans, Ali refers members to nutritionist Kamran Baig.

## Who can attend?
- **Plus and Elite:** free, book in the app up to 48 hours ahead (Elite 72 hours)
- **Standard members** (any home branch) and **non-members:** PKR 1,000 drop-in
- These are main-floor classes open to all adult members. Women on Standard, Plus or Elite are welcome.
- Student members can't attend, because all three classes fall outside the off-peak window (Mon–Fri 6:00am–4:00pm)

## Safety
Always use a spotter or the safety pins for heavy bench and squat. Dropping dumbbells from height is not allowed. Peak-time rack limits (30 minutes if someone is waiting, 6:00–9:00pm weekdays) don't apply to class participants during the class.
