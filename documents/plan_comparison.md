## Which plan should I choose?
- **Budget-conscious and training alone near home?** Standard. G-11 is the lowest price at PKR 8,000, but remember G-11 is closed on Sundays and has no steam or pool.
- **Two or more classes a week, or training at more than one branch?** Plus. Four drop-in classes on Standard (PKR 4,000) plus Standard at F-7 (PKR 12,000) already equals the Plus price.
- **Want regular coaching and a diet plan?** Elite. It includes 4 PT sessions and a consultation with Kamran Baig every month.
- **Prefer women-only spaces?** Ladies Only. If you also want the main floor, choose Standard, Plus or Elite instead, because female members on those plans can use both.

## Freeze allowance by term
Monthly plans cannot be frozen. 3-month plans get 7 days, 6-month plans 15 days, and 12-month plans 30 days. This applies to every plan type.
