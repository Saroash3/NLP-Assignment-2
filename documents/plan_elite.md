# Elite Membership Plan

Elite is Zor Fitness Club's all-inclusive plan. It combines access to every branch and class with monthly coaching, nutrition support, the pool and guest passes.

## Price
**PKR 26,000 per month**, the same at all branches.

| Term | Price | Admission fee |
|---|---|---|
| 1 month | PKR 26,000 | PKR 10,000 |
| 3 months | PKR 70,200 | PKR 10,000 |
| 6 months | PKR 132,600 | Waived |
| 12 months | PKR 234,000 | Waived |

## Everything in Plus, and:
- **4 personal-training sessions every month**, 60 minutes each, with any Zor trainer. Female members training on the Ladies Floor or Ladies Wing are coached by a female trainer.
- **1 nutrition consultation every month** with our registered dietitian **Kamran Baig**. Non-Elite members pay PKR 4,000 per consultation.
- **Pool access** at the F-7 Islamabad 20m heated pool in every session: men's session (Mon–Sat 6:00–9:00am and 7:00–10:00pm), the ladies session for female members (Mon–Sat 11:00am–3:00pm), and family swim on Sundays 10:00am–2:00pm
- **2 free guest passes every month.** Normally a guest pass is PKR 1,000 per visit.
- **Priority class booking**: book up to **72 hours** ahead, compared with 48 hours for other plans. This matters for the 24-bike spin studio, which fills fast.
- **One free towel per visit** at the front desk

## Important rules
- **The 4 monthly PT sessions do not roll over.** Sessions not used by the end of the month expire. Extra sessions can be bought as a PT package.
- **The 2 free guest passes do not roll over** either. The normal guest limits still apply: a maximum of 4 guest visits per member per month, the same guest at most twice a month, and guests cannot use the pool, steam or sauna.
- PT sessions follow the normal **12-hour cancellation notice**. A session cancelled late counts as used. If your trainer is more than 10 minutes late, you get that session free.
- The nutrition consultation must be booked in advance with Kamran Baig. He is at F-7 on Mon/Wed/Fri and at Bahria on Tue/Thu, 11:00am–7:00pm.

## Discounts on Elite
- **Couple:** 10% off each partner, i.e. PKR 23,400 per month each
- **Family** (3+ members of one household): 15% off each
- **Corporate:** 15% or 20% depending on company size
- The student discount does **not** apply to Elite. Only Standard and Plus qualify.

## Is Elite worth it?
Compare Elite with Plus plus add-ons. Plus costs PKR 16,000. Add an 8-session PT package every two months (PKR 18,000) and a monthly nutrition consultation (PKR 4,000), and you're already above Elite's PKR 26,000 before paying for a single pool swim (PKR 1,500) or guest pass (PKR 1,000). For anyone who wants regular coaching, Elite is the cheaper option.
