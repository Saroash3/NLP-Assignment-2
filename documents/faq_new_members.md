# FAQ: New Members

**Q: Can I try the gym before joining?**
A: Yes. First-time visitors get **one free trial day** plus a free InBody scan. It's one per CNIC. Book on WhatsApp (0333-5550967) or in the app, or just walk in with your CNIC.

**Q: What do I need to bring to join?**
A: Your **original CNIC** (16–17-year-olds bring a B-form plus a parent to sign), a passport photo (or we take one at the desk), and any discount proof, such as a student ID or nikahnama. You'll fill in the PAR-Q health form at the desk.

**Q: How much does it cost to join?**
A: Plan price plus a **one-time PKR 10,000 admission fee**. Standard is PKR 12,000 a month (PKR 8,000 at G-11), Plus PKR 16,000, Elite PKR 26,000 and Ladies Only PKR 10,000. The admission fee is **waived on 6-month and 12-month plans**.

**Q: Which plan is best for a beginner?**
A: If you'll train alone at one branch, **Standard**. If you want classes like Strength Foundations (Mon/Wed 7:00–8:00pm) to learn technique, **Plus**, because classes are included. If you want a coach every week, **Elite**, which includes 4 PT sessions a month.

**Q: Do I get any help on day one?**
A: Yes. A free **30-minute floor orientation** with a trainer, and an InBody scan.

**Q: I'm 15. Can I join?**
A: Ages **13–15** can join **supervised group classes only**, with a parent's signed consent. The gym floor is **16+**, and 16–17-year-olds need a parent's consent.

**Q: Is there a student discount?**
A: Yes, **25% off Standard or Plus** for ages 16–25 with a valid student ID. The catch is **off-peak entry only: Mon–Fri 6:00am–4:00pm**. For example, Standard at G-11 costs PKR 6,000 a month for students.

**Q: Can I change my mind after joining?**
A: Within **7 days**, if you **haven't visited even once** (we check the fingerprint log), you get the plan fee back. The PKR 10,000 admission fee is not refunded.

**Q: What should I wear?**
A: Athletic wear and **closed sports shoes**. **No jeans, chappals or khussa.** A **towel is mandatory** (rent one for PKR 300 if you forget). Shalwar kameez isn't allowed on the gym floor, but it's fine in yoga and Pilates.

**Q: How do I get in?**
A: By **fingerprint**. Never let anyone in on your fingerprint. The first time is a written warning, and the second time your membership is terminated.

**Q: Is there parking?**
A: F-7 has basement parking for 40 cars (free for 3 hours). Bahria has free open parking. G-11 has street parking only.

**Q: Does the membership renew automatically?**
A: No. There is **no auto-renewal**. The app reminds you 7 days before expiry, and there is a 5-day grace period after it.
