# Membership Freeze Policy

A freeze pauses your membership and moves your end date forward.

## Who can freeze?
**Monthly (1-month) plans cannot be frozen at all.** This applies to every plan type: Standard, Plus, Elite and Ladies Only. The Ramadan Fit Pass also cannot be frozen.

## Normal freeze allowance
| Plan term | Freeze allowance |
|---|---|
| 1 month | None |
| 3 months | 7 days |
| 6 months | 15 days |
| 12 months | 30 days |

## Freeze rules
- **Minimum 7 days per freeze.** You can't freeze for just 3 or 4 days.
- **Maximum 2 separate freezes per plan.** For example, a 12-month member could freeze 14 days in June and 16 days in December, but not three times.
- **Request at least 48 hours in advance** at the front desk, in the Zor Fitness app (Profile → Freeze), or on WhatsApp 0333-5550967
- **No backdating.** You can't freeze days that have already passed, even if you were travelling or ill. Tell us before you go.
- Unused freeze days **do not carry over** to your next plan and can't be exchanged for money

## While you're frozen
- **You cannot enter any branch**, not even as someone's guest
- Class bookings made for the frozen period are cancelled automatically, and they don't count as no-shows
- Elite members' monthly PT sessions and guest passes for the frozen period are **not** added back
- Your end date moves forward by exactly the number of frozen days
- If Eid closures fall during or after your freeze, those days are still added automatically

## Medical freeze
- Up to **60 days**, **free**, with a **doctor's certificate**
- On plans of **3 months or longer** only
- It is **in addition to** your normal allowance
- Common uses: surgery recovery, fractures, dengue or typhoid recovery, late pregnancy and delivery
- It can be requested with less than 48 hours' notice in an emergency. Send the certificate on WhatsApp within 7 days.

## Student exam freeze
Student-discount members on 3-month-plus plans get **one free 30-day exam freeze per year**, with an official **date sheet**. It is in addition to the normal allowance.

## Suspended members can't freeze
If your 12-month second instalment is unpaid for more than 15 days, your entry is suspended. You **cannot freeze** until the balance and the PKR 1,500 late fee are cleared. Suspended days are not added back.

## Ramadan is not a freeze
The gym stays open in Ramadan with special hours. Members who don't want to train must use their normal allowance.

## Examples
- A 6-month member travelling to Umrah for 12 days: freeze 12 days (within 15), requested 48 hours before departure.
- A 3-month member going home to Multan for 10 days: only 7 days are allowed, so they freeze 7 and lose 3.
