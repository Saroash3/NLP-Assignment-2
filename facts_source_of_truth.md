## 9. Policies
- **Refunds:** fees are **non-refundable**, except:
  1. **7-day cooling-off:** a full refund of the plan fee (**admission fee NOT refunded**) if requested within 7 days of joining **and no visits have been made** (checked by biometric log).
  2. **Relocation:** moving **>50 km from every branch**, on 6- or 12-month plans only → pro-rata refund of unused **full months** minus a **10% processing fee**; proof of the new address (utility bill or employer letter).
  3. **Medical inability** (permanent, doctor-certified) on 6/12-month plans → the same as relocation.
  - Processed within **14 working days** to the original payment method (cash payments are refunded by bank transfer).
  - PT packages, day passes, drop-ins and café purchases are never refunded.
- **Freeze:** monthly plans **cannot** be frozen. Allowance: **3-month 7 days, 6-month 15 days, 12-month 30 days**; **minimum 7 days per freeze**; max **2 separate freezes per plan**. Request **48h ahead** (desk, app or WhatsApp); **no backdating**. **Medical freeze:** up to **60 days** with a doctor's certificate on 3-month+ plans, free, in addition to the allowance. **Student exam freeze:** see §4. Frozen members cannot enter, even as a guest. Eid closure days are automatically added.
- **Cancellation:** monthly plans — no contract, simply don't renew. Prepaid plans — refund policy only. **Class cancellation notice: 3 hours; PT cancellation notice: 12 hours.**
- **Transfer:** personal, non-transferable. **Exception:** a 12-month plan may be transferred **once** to an immediate family member (spouse, parent, child, sibling) for **3,000**; at least **3 months must remain**.
