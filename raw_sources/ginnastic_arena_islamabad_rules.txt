Source: https://ginnasticarena.com/ginnastic-arena-gym-rules/
Accessed: 2026-09-30
City: Islamabad, Interlace Plaza, I-8 Markaz (open 24/7)

Rules:
- Suitable exercise attire is required; management can ask improperly dressed members to leave.
- Guests: members 16+ may bring guests aged 16+ (18+ if the member is under 18); guests pay and complete a health
  commitment statement; the member must accompany the guest and is responsible for their conduct.
- Frozen members cannot enter, even as a guest, until the freeze ends.
- Bags, jackets and rucksacks go in lockers, not on the gym floor.
- Steam/sauna/jacuzzi/spa close 15 minutes before the advertised closing time.
- Prohibited: foul or loud language, harassment, drugs, alcohol, smoking/e-cigarettes.
- Replacement membership card: Rs. 1,000. Guest fee varies.
- Non-compliance: ejection or membership termination.
Missing: refund and transfer rules.
